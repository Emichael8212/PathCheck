# M1 check — both flows on phone and desktop

**Status: waiting for the owner.** M2 is not started before your answer, which is then
recorded in `docs/decision-log.md` under "Milestone checks".

M1 is "Flow A + Flow B with synthetic transcripts, on localhost". All of it runs on your
own computer with made-up data: nothing is public and nothing is sent anywhere.

## 1. How to open it

**On your computer**

1. In the project folder run `./scripts/init.sh`.
2. Open http://127.0.0.1:5173/ in your browser.

**On your phone** (same Wi-Fi as the computer)

1. Stop the server from the step above if it is running, then run `npm run dev:lan`.
2. It prints a line like `Network: http://192.168.1.23:5173/`. Type that address into the
   phone's browser.
3. Send the two made-up PDFs to the phone (AirDrop, or email them to yourself):
   `synthetic/transcripts/sample-a.pdf` and `synthetic/transcripts/sample-b.pdf`.
4. When you are done, stop the server with Ctrl+C. `npm run dev:lan` makes this project's
   folder readable by other devices on your Wi-Fi while it runs; it is for this check
   only. Don't save your real transcript inside the project folder while it is running.

If you'd rather not use the phone itself, the desktop browser's phone view (in Chrome:
View → Developer → Developer Tools → the phone icon) shows the same pages at phone width.
The file picker and the print window are the two things only a real phone can show.

**Only the two made-up PDFs can be read at M1.** Your real transcript will be refused with
"PathCheck can only read its two made-up transcripts for now. Nothing from this file was
kept or sent." Reading real transcripts is M2.

## 2. What to check

### Flow A: "Check my courses"

1. Open the site and choose **Check my courses**. Type `MGT 321` and press Enter.
   You should see "Counts toward CIS — Business core" with "unless you've already passed
   it or filled this slot" under it, and "3 Total credits" above with the note "Below 12
   credits: below full-time; may affect scholarships."
2. Add `BIO 230`. You should see "Doesn't count toward CIS — not in the CIS curriculum".
3. Add `FLS 161`. The row should ask "Are you a legacy CIS student?". Answer Yes: it
   should now count as an elective, and "Legacy CIS student: Yes Change" appears under
   the list.
4. Click **Choose transcript PDF**, pick `sample-a.pdf`. A page shows what was read; click
   **Continue**.
   You should be back on "Check my courses" with "Transcript added". MGT 321 no longer
   says "unless …". Add `ENG 131`: "You already passed this".
5. Under the three numbers, one line should say when you finish at this pace, with
   "Credits only — course order may add semesters." under it.

### Flow B: "See what I have left"

1. Choose **What I have left**. With `sample-a.pdf` already added, the result is there:
   **81 credits left** and **On pace for Spring 2029**, about 16 credits a term. (On a
   fresh start: Browse files → `sample-a.pdf` → Continue.)
2. Under "Requirements" there should be nine, each Done, In progress or Still needed.
   Open one to see its courses. "Not done" hides the finished ones.
3. Under "Draft plan" there should be five semesters, Spring 2027 to Spring 2029, with
   18, 18, 15, 15 and 15 credits, and MGT 462 last with "Always the final semester".
   Click **Move later** on MGT 321: it goes to the next semester and the others fill in.
4. Click **Print / save PDF for your advisor**. The preview should start with "PathCheck
   summary — …", show every requirement opened, and show no buttons.
5. Reload the page: the same result should still be there. Then click **Clear my data**:
   the upload page returns with "Your data was cleared from this browser."

### A second student

After step 5 above nothing is kept. On "What I have left": Browse files → `sample-b.pdf` →
Continue. Before the checklist it should ask "Are you a legacy CIS student?". With Yes:
**89 credits left** and **On pace for Spring 2028**. "Change" above the result asks
again; with No: 92 credits left.

### Things only you can check

- **On a real iPhone:** that "Browse files" opens the file picker, that the credits
  dropdown of an unknown course doesn't zoom the page, and that the print window opens.
- **In Safari and Firefox:** only Chrome's engine was available on the build machine.
- **On paper:** print one summary on a real printer.

### Rules I read into your answers

- **Pace** (PR #22): on pace means the credits left fit into the terms up to the target
  at the most credits per term; the fewest credits changes only the detail line; the
  first target offered is the earliest one reachable; summers use the same range.
- **The pace line on "Check my courses"** (PR #23) uses only the credits that count toward
  CIS, and Fall and Spring only.
- **The draft plan** (PR #24): ordered by level, which moves twelve entries compared with
  your screenshot (you approved the rule; `docs/design/DESIGN.md` lists each); a semester
  can be under the fewest credits when no remaining course fits; moving a course out of
  the last semester takes MGT 462 with it.
- **The 12-month banner** (PR #27) appears from 2027-11-16, the day after the anniversary
  of your review, by the date on the student's device.
- **A course outside the curriculum** keeps "Doesn't count" even when the transcript has
  it passed. "You already passed this", "This slot is already filled" and "Already in
  progress." are drawn like "Doesn't count". (PR #20)
- **Which of two interchangeable courses is shown as filling a place** (PR #9): a passed
  course before one in progress; HIS 132 or ENG 210 first for their picks.
- **The sequence is judged on its two courses themselves** (PR #9): HIS 132 in progress
  shows the sequence as in progress, even when another passed course holds the pick.
- **Where PSY 251 goes for a legacy student** (PR #11) is my reading of the brief's "place
  it where it helps most": it only chooses between the two places the curriculum gives
  it.
- **Grades and attempts** (PR #6): a grade that isn't on the A–F scale never counts and is
  not called "must be repeated"; a course with several attempts and none passed shows
  as in progress if one is still running, and as "must be repeated" only when nothing
  leaves the outcome open.
- **A transcript with only transfer rows** has no term to count from, so it gets "Pace
  can't be estimated…", although nothing was misread. (PR #22)
- **The review step** (PR #19): every row can be edited, not only the faulty ones;
  "Continue" is allowed while rows still need fixing (they count nothing); one row is
  edited at a time. The review opens under "What I have left" in the top bar even when
  the PDF was chosen on "Check my courses" (PR #20).
- **Remembering** (PR #28): a transcript still in its first review is not kept; two tabs
  don't follow each other.
