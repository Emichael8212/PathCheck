# F19 — Remembering: the last result stays only in the student's browser, with "Clear my data"

Milestone: M1 · Branch: `feat/F19-remember-clear` · Brief sections: §4 "Remembering", §7, §2

## What the student gets
A student who comes back finds what they left: their result on "See what I have left" and
their list on "Check my courses", without bringing the transcript again. It is kept in
their own browser and nowhere else, and one button, "Clear my data", removes all of it.

## User-visible change
Yes.

## What is built
- **What is kept**, in the browser's own storage (localStorage) and only there:
  - the last result of "See what I have left", as little of the transcript as the
    result needs: for each reviewed row the course, its printed title, the term, the
    credits, whether it is transfer credit, and which rows were left out; of the grade
    only what the result uses: **a passing grade is kept as "Passed", never as its
    letter**; a course in progress as in progress; a grade that does not count (below
    the lowest counting grade, or a mark such as W or I) as it is, because the result
    says it ("Repeat; the earlier D doesn't count."); a row that still needs fixing as
    it was read, because it has to be fixed. Totals lines of the transcript (GPA,
    standing) are not kept at all;
  - with it, the pace settings and the courses moved in the draft plan;
  - the list of "Check my courses" (each course, with the credits the student gave a
    course PathCheck doesn't know) and the term chosen for its pace line;
  - the legacy answer (as since F05).
- **What is never kept**: the PDF, the file's name (it can hold the student's name), an
  edit that was not saved, which requirements were opened, "All / Not done", the day.
  A transcript still in its first review is not kept either: there is no result yet.
- **When it is kept**: the result, each time it changes after "Looks right — continue"
  (a correction saved and continued, a pace setting, a move, the legacy answer); the
  list, each time it changes. Going back to "Check what was read" keeps the last result
  until the student continues again.
- **Coming back** (a reload, a new tab, the next day): "See what I have left" shows the
  result again, as it was left; "Check my courses" shows the list with its answers, and
  uses the transcript again ("Transcript added"). Where the page named the file, it
  says "your transcript". "Check what was read" still works: the rows are there, with
  "Passed" where a passing letter was. A row can still be corrected (its grade is then
  chosen again, or left "Passed"), left out or put back. The result is the same as
  before the return in every word and number.
- **What is kept is not trusted.** On return it goes through the same checks as a fresh
  transcript and a typed course: rows that are no longer valid show as rows to fix, a
  code that is not a course code is dropped, settings out of range fall back to the
  usual ones. Something that cannot be read at all is thrown away, and the student
  starts fresh; nothing fails.
- **Said on the page**: with the button, one sentence: "This result stays in this browser
  until you clear it." (on "Check my courses": "This list stays in this browser until you
  clear it."), so that a student on a shared computer knows. Not shown where the
  browser cannot keep anything.
- **"Clear my data"**: the design's secondary button with its trash icon, beside "Print /
  save PDF for your advisor" at the end of the result. Also at the end of "Check my
  courses", and on "See what I have left" before there is a result, whenever anything is
  kept or shown there (a course in the list, a transcript, a legacy answer). Pressing it
  removes everything PathCheck stored in the browser, the legacy answer included,
  forgets the same things on the page, and says "Your data was cleared from this
  browser." on the page and to a screen reader. The page is then as on a first visit.
  It asks nothing first: the result comes back by choosing the PDF again; corrections
  made in the review are lost with it and have to be made again.
- **Where storage cannot be used** (private browsing, site data blocked, storage full):
  nothing is remembered and nothing fails; the pages work for the visit as before.
  "Clear my data" still forgets what is on the page.
- **Nothing leaves the browser.** PathCheck has no account, no sign-in, no cookie and no
  server piece at M1; reading a made-up transcript happens in the browser. No request
  carries anything of the student's.

## Decided here (recorded in `docs/decision-log.md`)
- **"The last result" is kept as what the result needs of the reviewed rows, plus the
  settings**, not as a picture of the page: the result is drawn again by the same code,
  so it can never differ from a fresh one, and "Check what was read" keeps working.
- **No passing letter grade is kept.** The result never shows one (an A and a C are both
  "Done"), so keeping it would keep the transcript, not the result, and the page's
  sentence "Your PDF is read, then deleted. Only your result stays in this browser."
  would stop being true. On a shared computer the next person finds no grade that the
  result itself does not say. (Evaluator, contract round 1; no source allows more.)
- **A row that still needs fixing is kept as it was read**, its grade included: it is not
  part of the result (it counts nothing until fixed), the note above the result says so,
  and the student needs what was read to fix it. It leaves the entry when the row is
  fixed, left out or cleared. (Evaluator, contract round 2.)
- **"Passed" is read back only from what PathCheck itself kept.** A freshly read
  transcript that prints "Passed" as a grade is still a row to fix, as today: no rule
  about such a grade is made here.
- **The file's name is not kept** (brief §7: no personal data is stored anywhere).
- **A first review that was not continued is not kept.**
- **The list of "Check my courses" is kept too** (it is that flow's result).
- **"Clear my data" asks no confirmation**, and is offered wherever something is kept,
  not only on the result (the design shows it there).
- **One storage entry beside the legacy answer** (`pathcheck.visit`), with a version; an
  unknown version is thrown away.
- **Two tabs**: the tab that changed something last is what is kept; tabs do not follow
  each other.
- **The term of the pace line** is kept with the list, and reset with a new transcript,
  as since F15.
- **Wording by the agent**, listed in the pull request: "Your data was cleared from this
  browser."; "your transcript" in the place of the file's name; "Passed" in the place of
  a passing letter; "This result stays in this browser until you clear it." and "This
  list stays in this browser until you clear it."

## Acceptance checks
Tests: `tests/remember.test.ts` (`remember`: what is stored and read back, as text and in
the simulated browser), `tests/print.test.ts` and `tests/left-page.test.ts` (existing, where
the actions row is pinned). "Evaluator": a real browser on the development server, 1440
and 375, light and dark, with reloads and a second tab.

1. **The result comes back.** `sample-a`, continue, then: most credits 19, Summers on, MGT
   321 moved later. After a reload "See what I have left" shows the same result: the
   same credits, requirements, pace answer and settings, the plan with MGT 321 where it
   was moved; no upload is asked for. — `remember` "shows the last result again";
   evaluator (a real reload; a new tab).
2. **The list comes back.** MGT 321, BIO 230 and a course PathCheck doesn't know with its
   credits changed: after a reload "Check my courses" shows the three with the same
   answers and credits, and with the transcript of check 1 the answers are the exact
   ones ("Transcript added") and the pace line has the term that was chosen for it. — `remember` "shows the list again"; evaluator.
3. **As it was left, not as it was read.** A corrected row and a row left out are still
   corrected and left out after a reload; a row still to fix is still to fix (with its
   note); a legacy student's answer is still given and can still be changed; "Check what
   was read" opens the rows. — `remember` "keeps the corrections".
4. **Only a result is kept.** A transcript chosen but not continued is gone after a reload
   (the upload page again); a result, then "Check what was read", then a reload: the
   last result is back; "Use another transcript": nothing of the old one is kept. —
   `remember` "keeps a result, not a review in progress".
5. **What is kept, exactly.** After checks 1 to 3 the browser's storage holds two entries
   of PathCheck's (`pathcheck.visit`, `pathcheck.legacy`) and nothing else of its making:
   no cookie, nothing in sessionStorage. The visit entry holds no file name, none of
   the PDF, no totals line (GPA, standing), no credits earned or quality points, and
   **no passing letter grade**: every passing row has "Passed", every row in progress
   has no grade, and the only other grades in it are those that do not count and those
   of rows still to fix, as they were read. — `remember` "keeps only what the result
   needs"; evaluator (the storage read in the browser's tools). It
   has the rows, the settings, the moves and the list.
14. **The same result without the letters.** For `sample-a` and `sample-b` (and a
    transcript with a D that was repeated, a W, transfer credit and a row to fix): the
    result page, word for word and number for number, is the same before and after a
    reload, "Repeat; the earlier D doesn't count." included; after the reload "Check what
    was read" shows "Passed" where a passing letter was, the non-counting grades as
    they were, and a row opened for editing and saved unchanged is still "Passed"; a
    freshly read row whose grade is printed "Passed" is still a row to fix. The row to
    fix has its grade kept as read ("B+"); once it is fixed the entry has "Passed" (or
    the non-counting grade chosen), and once it is left out or the data is cleared it
    is gone. — `remember`
    "keeps no passing grade and shows the same result", "keeps every fault of a row that
    still needs fixing"; evaluator (the storage read in the browser; the two pages
    compared).
6. **Clear my data.** The button is at the end of the result beside the print button
   (secondary, trash icon), with "This result stays in this browser until you clear
   it." (not shown where nothing can be kept); at the end of "Check my courses" when the list has a course,
   a transcript is in use or a legacy answer is given; on "See what I have left" before a
   result when a legacy answer or a list is kept; not on Start; not on a first visit
   with nothing kept. Pressing it: storage has no entry of PathCheck's; the page says
   "Your data was cleared from this browser."; the list is empty, the upload page is
   back, the legacy question is asked again when a legacy course is typed; after a
   reload nothing comes back. Where the browser cannot print, the button is still
   there. — `remember` "clears everything"; `print` "offers no print where the browser
   has none" (extended); evaluator.
7. **Not trusted.** With the stored entry replaced by: text that is not JSON; another
   version; rows that are not rows; a row's printed title, a moved entry's name or a
   list holding `<img src=x onerror=…>`; 5,000 entries; settings of −3 and 9,999 or a
   target that is not a term; a moved entry that is not in the plan — the pages draw without an error, nothing of it is drawn as HTML, bad parts
   are dropped or fall back, and the entry is rewritten clean or removed. — `remember`
   "trusts nothing it reads back".
8. **Without storage.** With storage missing, throwing on read, or throwing on write
   (full): both flows work for the visit, nothing is said about an error, "Clear my
   data" forgets what is on the page. — `remember` "works without storage".
9. **Nothing leaves the browser.** Through checks 1 to 6 no request is made (no fetch, no
   XMLHttpRequest, no beacon, no WebSocket), no cookie is set, and no page has a sign-in
   or an account. The built site contains no call that could send data. — `remember`
   "sends nothing anywhere"; `scripts/check-build.mjs` (unchanged: still passes);
   evaluator (the network panel during a whole visit: only the site's own files).
10. **With the rest.** The banner still follows the day on return (nothing about it is
    kept); the printed summary of a result that came back is the same paper; "Report a
    mistake" on a result that came back is filled in as before; "Clear my data" is not
    printed. — `remember` "comes back whole"; evaluator.
11. **Looks like the design.** The two buttons at the end of the result as in the design
    (primary, secondary; they wrap on the phone); the cleared note readable (AA);
    nothing overlaps at 375 and 320; no console error. — evaluator.
12. **Records.** Decision log; DESIGN.md (the button's other places; the cleared note);
    `_carried-forward.md` (F19's notes removed; for F21 and M2: what is kept when real
    transcripts are read, and the consent sentence must say it); `features.json` F19.
13. `npm run check` passes.

## Out of scope
- The upload function and its "keeps nothing" on the server (F21 and F22, M2): at M1 no
  server piece exists. Accounts of any kind (never).
- Keeping more than the last result, or a history.
