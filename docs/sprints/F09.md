## What the student gets
Above the course list, three numbers: the total credits of the courses entered, the credits
that count toward CIS, and the credits that don't. A neutral note appears when the total is
under 12 or over 18. For a course PathCheck doesn't know, the student can change the 3
credits it starts with, and the numbers follow.

## User-visible change
Yes.

## What is built
- `src/credits.ts`: the totals of a list of courses with their answers (total, credits
  that count, credits that don't), and which note applies.
- On the page (`src/check.ts`): the design's summary strip between the heading and the
  field, drawn only when the list has a course; and, on the row of a course PathCheck
  doesn't know, a small control to change its credits.

## The rules
- **Total credits** is the sum of the credits of every course in the list: the
  curriculum's for a listed course, the elective rule's for a course that is only an
  elective by the rule, and the student's number (3 to start with) for a course PathCheck
  doesn't know.
- **Count toward CIS** is the sum for the courses whose answer is a "Counts"; **Don't
  count** is the sum for the courses whose answer is a "Doesn't count". A course that
  PathCheck doesn't know never counts, so its credits are only ever in the total and in
  "Don't count".
- A row that shows the legacy question, or waits for it, is in the total and in neither
  of the other two until the question is answered. While that is so, the strip says under
  the two numbers that they are waiting for the legacy answer.
- Without a transcript the "Count toward CIS" number has under it "unless you've already
  passed them or filled their slots" (owner answer b).
- **Notes.** Under 12 credits in total (a total below 12): "Below 12 credits: below full-time; may affect
  scholarships." Over 18: "Over 18 credits: may cost extra." From 12 to 18: no note. These
  are the sentences of the approved design's pace controls, which contain the brief's.

## Decided here (recorded in `docs/decision-log.md`)
- **Where the note goes.** Under the "Total credits" label, in the strip, as plain text
  (no banner, no color of alarm): the design has no place for it on this page.
- **The control for an unknown course's credits** is a small dropdown in the row's credits
  cell, labelled "Credits for <code>", with the whole numbers 0 to 12 and the default
  selected. A dropdown cannot hold a wrong value, works with the keyboard and on a phone,
  and fits the cell; 12 leaves room for a large block such as an internship, so the total
  is not understated. (The design shows "3" as text; this control is the difference the
  owner's answer of 2026-11-16 asks for, recorded in DESIGN.md.) Courses PathCheck knows
  show their credits as text, as now.
- **Nothing is drawn for an empty list**: no strip and no note. A list with one 3-credit
  course shows the under-12 note, as the brief says.
- **The waiting line under the two numbers** while the legacy question is unanswered and
  matters: "Waiting for your legacy answer." (agent's wording; listed in the pull
  request).
- **12 and 18** are the brief's numbers for a full-time load and the usual most; they are
  constants in `src/credits.ts`, not curriculum data.

## Acceptance checks
Tests are in `tests/credits.test.ts` (the rules), `tests/check.test.ts` (the page's text) and
`tests/check-page.test.ts` (the page in a simulated browser). "Evaluator" means checked in
a real browser at 1440 and 375, light and dark, against the screenshots.

1. **The total.** With ACT 215, MGT 321, MKT 410, BIO 230, ENG 131 and ENG 140 the strip
   shows 18 "Total credits", 12 "Count toward CIS" and 6 "Don't count", as in the design.
   ORI 100 alone shows 1. A course that only matches the elective rule (MKT 410) adds the
   rule's 3. — `credits` "adds up the credits of the entered courses", "adds the rule's
   credits for an elective by the rule".
2. **Under 12.** 1, 3, 9 and 11 credits show "Below 12 credits: below full-time; may affect
   scholarships." and no other note; the sentence contains the brief's words "below
   full-time; may affect scholarships". — "notes a total under 12".
3. **Over 18.** 19 and 21 credits show "Over 18 credits: may cost extra." and no other
   note; the sentence contains the brief's words "may cost extra". — "notes a total over
   18".
4. **From 12 to 18.** 12, 15 and 18 credits show no note. — "has no note from 12 to 18".
5. **What counts and what doesn't.** The two numbers follow the answers of F08 exactly:
   a second course for a filled pick, a fourth elective, CIS 304, BIO 230 and a legacy
   course on No are in "Don't count"; over many random lists, for Yes, No and no answer:
   total = counts + doesn't count + the credits of the rows that show the question or
   wait (none once the answer is given); "counts" is the sum of the credits of the rows
   that say "Counts"; and the waiting line is shown exactly when such a row exists.
   — "splits the total by the answers", "adds up for any list".
6. **A course PathCheck doesn't know starts at 3 and can be changed.** BIO 230 is added
   with 3 credits in a dropdown labelled "Credits for BIO 230"; so are CIS 304 and
   CIS 415. Choosing 4 makes the total and "Don't count" each go up by 1 at once, without
   the row or the field being redrawn (the keyboard stays on the dropdown). The dropdown
   offers 0 to 12; choosing 12 for BIO 230 beside MGT 321, ENG 131 and ACT 215 makes the
   total 21 and shows the over-18 note. — `credits` "starts an unknown course at 3 credits"; `check-page`
   "changes the total when an unknown course's credits are changed".
7. **Courses PathCheck knows cannot be edited.** A listed course (MGT 321, ORI 100,
   FLS 161) and an elective by the rule (MKT 410) show their credits as text; no row but
   an unknown course's has a control in its credits cell. — `check` "lets only an unknown
   course's credits be changed".
8. **An unknown course's credits never count toward CIS.** With MGT 321 and BIO 230:
   counts 3; changing BIO 230 to 9 leaves "Count toward CIS" at 3 and takes "Don't count"
   to 9 and the total to 12. Over random lists and credit values, changing an unknown
   course's credits never changes "Count toward CIS". — `credits` "never counts an
   unknown course's credits"; `check-page` (the same in the page).
9. **An edited value moves the notes.** MGT 321, ENG 131, ACT 215 (9) and BIO 230 at 3
   make 12: no note; BIO 230 at 2 makes 11: the under-12 note; back to 3: no note.
   MGT 321, ENG 131, ACT 215, MKT 321, CIS 300 and BIO 230 at 3 make 18: no note; BIO 230
   at 4: the over-18 note.
   — `check-page` "moves the notes when an unknown course's credits are changed".
10. **The condition under the count.** "unless you've already passed them or filled their
    slots" is under "Count toward CIS" whenever the strip is shown; the number is never
    shown without it. — `check` "never shows the count without its condition".
11. **Waiting for the legacy answer.** FLS 161 and MGT 321 with no answer: total 6, counts
    3, doesn't count 0, and "Waiting for your legacy answer." under the two numbers. After
    Yes: 6 / 6 / 0 and the line is gone; after No: 6 / 3 / 3. A waiting row (ECO 301 after
    FLS 161 and two electives) is treated the same. — `credits` "leaves a course that
    waits for the legacy answer out of both counts"; `check-page` "follows the legacy
    answer in the numbers".
12. **The strip follows the list.** Adding, removing, answering the legacy question,
    "Change" and the filter each leave the numbers right; the "Doesn't count" filter does
    not change them (they are about the whole list). The strip is absent for an empty
    list and comes back with the first course. The changed credits of an unknown course
    are kept while the student moves between pages, and are back to 3 if the course is
    removed and added again. — `check-page` "keeps the numbers right as the list changes".
13. **Screen reader and keyboard.** The strip is a labelled group ("Credits"); each number
    is read with its label; a note is read when it appears (a polite status), and not
    read again on every change that keeps it. The dropdown is reachable by keyboard and
    has a visible focus outline. — `check` "labels the numbers"; evaluator.
14. **It looks like the approved design.** With the six courses of the screenshot the
    strip matches `check-*-*.png` at 1440 and 375, light and dark: three cells, the
    numbers in the design's colors, the middle cell wider on the phone so the condition
    fits. Differences listed in the pull request: the dropdown on an unknown course's row;
    the note line and the waiting line when they apply. Contrast meets WCAG AA. At 375
    nothing is hidden or overlapping, including a row with the dropdown. — evaluator.
15. **No curriculum fact in the app's source; nothing leaves the browser.** The scans
    pass. — the scans.
16. **Records.** `features.json`: F09 `passes: true` with evidence and tests.
    `docs/decision-log.md` has the "Decided here" entries, and names the source of the
    two note sentences (the approved design's pace controls). `docs/design/DESIGN.md` records
    the credits control, the note line and the waiting line as differences with their
    source. `docs/sprints/_carried-forward.md`: the F09 notes that are dealt with are
    removed; for F12, that with a transcript the condition under the count is dropped
    (owner answer b) and the numbers come from the exact answers; for F15, where the
    semester's credits come from; for F19, that changed credits are part of what is kept
    and cleared. — the files; reviewer.
17. **`npm run check` passes**, and the built site shows the strip with no console error.
    — evaluator.

## Out of scope
- Anything with a transcript: exact counts without the condition (F12), the pace line
  (F15).
- Keeping the list and the changed credits across visits (F19).
- Any limit on how many credits may be taken: PathCheck sets none; the portal does.
