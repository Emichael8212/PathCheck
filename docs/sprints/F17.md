# F17 — Print / PDF summary for an advisor

Milestone: M1 · Branch: `feat/F17-print-summary` · Brief sections: §4 Flow B step 6

## What the student gets
At the end of "See what I have left" a button prints the result, or saves it as a PDF,
as a summary the student can take to an advisor: what counts, what is left, the pace and
the draft plan, on plain white paper, without the app's buttons.

## User-visible change
Yes.

## What is built
- **The button.** After the draft plan, the design's primary button "Print / save PDF for
  your advisor" with its print icon. It opens the browser's own print window, where the
  student prints or chooses "Save as PDF". Nothing is made or sent by PathCheck: the
  browser does the printing. The button is there whenever the result is (so not in the
  review step, not before the legacy answer, not with no row taken on). In a browser
  that cannot print (some in-app browsers have no print) the button is not drawn: no
  button that does nothing. "Clear my data", which sits beside it in the design's
  `.actions` row, comes with F19.
- **While printing, the tab is named "PathCheck summary"**, so that "Save as PDF" suggests
  that as the file's name; afterwards it has its usual name again.
- **What is on paper** (the same result, as it stands on the screen at that moment):
  - a line at the top, on paper only, that says what this is: "PathCheck summary —
    <program>, <institution>" (both from the curriculum file) and under it "Not an
    official degree audit. Not affiliated with Alabama State University. Curriculum last
    reviewed: Nov 2026" (the footer's three sentences, from the same code): the paper
    names the university, so it says at once that it is not the university's;
  - the heading, the credits ring and bar, the pace answer and its caveat;
  - one line, on paper only, in the place of the four pace controls, saying the settings
    the estimate and the plan were made with: "Target semester: Spring 2029. Fewest
    credits per term: 12 credits. Most credits per term: 18 credits. Summers: Off." (the controls'
    own labels and values);
  - every requirement **opened**, done ones included, whatever the student had opened or
    closed and whether "All" or "Not done" was chosen; the sequence box; the courses that
    do not count, opened;
  - the draft plan as the student left it, moved courses included, with its label "Draft
    — Order not checked — your portal checks prerequisites" and the line under it when it
    ends later than the pace estimate;
  - the notes that qualify the result: the rows-still-to-fix note, the legacy line
    ("Legacy CIS student: Yes"), "17 rows taken on.";
  - the footer: "Not an official degree audit.", "Curriculum last reviewed: Nov 2026",
    "Not affiliated with Alabama State University".
- **What is not on paper** (app controls): the top bar; "All / Not done"; "Show courses" /
  "Hide courses"; "Move later"; the pace controls; "Change" beside the legacy line; "Fix
  it"; "Check what was read", "Use another transcript" and the sentence about it; the
  print button itself; the "Report a mistake" link (a link does nothing on paper; F18
  owns it on screen).
- **After printing** the page is as it was: the requirements the student had closed are
  closed again, the chosen filter still applies, the keyboard is on the print button.
- The browser's own "Print" (the menu or the keyboard shortcut) gives the same paper as
  the button.

## How "everything opened" is made sure
No single signal from the browser is relied on:
- **The button prepares the page itself** before it calls the browser's print: it opens
  every requirement and the list of courses that do not count, and shows the cards that
  "Not done" hides. It puts the page back when the print call returns and when the
  browser says printing has ended, whichever comes first, and only once.
- **The browser's own Print** prepares the page when the browser says printing starts
  (`beforeprint`, and the print media query turning on) and puts it back when it ends
  (`afterprint`, the query turning off).
- **The stylesheet's print rules** show a requirement card hidden by "Not done" whatever
  happens, and, where the browser supports it, print the inside of a closed requirement
  (`::details-content`). So a path on which no signal comes still prints every
  requirement's card, and in current browsers its courses.
- If the browser never says that printing ended, the page stays all-open: nothing is
  lost, and the next change of the filter or a card works as usual.

## Decided here (recorded in `docs/decision-log.md`)
- **The summary is the result page printed**, not a second document: one source, so the
  paper can never say something else than the screen.
- **The lines every result carries are also at the top of the paper**, not only in the
  footer at the end: an advisor reads the top first. With them, "Not affiliated with
  <institution>", because the line above names the institution. The design has no place
  for these paper-only lines or for the settings line: listed in DESIGN.md and in the
  pull request.
- **No print button where the browser cannot print.**
- **The tab's name while printing** is "PathCheck summary" (the agent's words).
- **The pace settings are printed as one line**, since the controls that show them on
  the screen are not printed and the plan depends on them.
- **"Report a mistake" is not printed** (the brief has the link on every result; on paper
  it is a word that does nothing; listed in the pull request).
- **Paper is white with dark text whatever the device's colour mode**; the ring, the bars
  and the tinted rows are printed (the design's print rules, already in the stylesheet).
- **Nothing is cut**: a requirement card, a semester and a plan entry are not split over
  two pages when they fit on one; a heading is not left alone at the foot of a page.
- **Wording by the agent**, listed in the pull request: "PathCheck summary — <program>,
  <institution>". Everything else is the design's or already on the page.
- Flow A ("Check my courses") has no print button: the brief has it in Flow B only.

## Acceptance checks
Tests: `tests/print.test.ts` (`print`: the page as text and the simulated browser),
`tests/left-page.test.ts` (existing tests, where the result's text is pinned). "Evaluator":
a real browser on the development server: the print layout (print media emulated) and a
PDF made by the browser, Letter and A4, from a light and a dark device.

1. **The button.** With the result: one button "Print / save PDF for your advisor", after
   the draft plan, with the print icon; none in the review step, before the legacy
   answer, with no row taken on, or on the other pages; none when the browser has no
   print. It sits in the design's `.actions` row (where F19 adds "Clear my data"). —
   `print` "offers print with the result", "offers no print where the browser has
   none"; evaluator.
2. **It prints.** Pressing it opens the browser's print window once; the keyboard stays on
   the button; the tab is named "PathCheck summary" while the print window is open and
   has its own name again after; nothing is sent anywhere (no request is made). — `print` "opens the
   browser's print window"; evaluator (the print call observed; no network request).
3. **Everything is opened on paper**, also with "Not done" chosen and with cards the
   student closed:
   - the button: at the moment the browser's print is called, every requirement and the
     list of courses that do not count are open and no card is hidden; after the call
     returns the page is as it was (closed again, hidden again, the filter's buttons
     unchanged), and a later `afterprint` changes nothing more — `print` "opens every
     requirement before it prints and puts the page back once";
   - the browser's own Print: the same on `beforeprint` / `afterprint`, and on the print
     media query turning on and off — `print` "does the same for the browser's own
     Print";
   - no signal at all: the stylesheet's print rules show a card with `hidden` and the
     inside of a closed requirement — `print` "prints filtered cards whatever happens"
     (the rules are in the stylesheet's print block);
   - evaluator, through the real paths: (a) press the button with the browser's print
     replaced by a function that records the page at that moment (every `details` open,
     no card hidden) and makes the PDF from print media, then check the page was put
     back; (b) dispatch `beforeprint`, make the PDF, dispatch `afterprint`, check the
     page was put back; (c) with "Not done" chosen and no event at all, make the PDF:
     every requirement's card is in it. In (a) and (b) the PDF's text holds a course of
     a done requirement (ORI 100).
4. **The lines at the top.** The paper starts with "PathCheck summary — B.S. Computer
   Information Systems, Alabama State University" and "Not an official degree audit. Not
   affiliated with Alabama State University. Curriculum last reviewed: Nov 2026", and
   ends with the footer's same sentences; on the screen the top lines are not shown.
   With a curriculum that has no review date the second line is "Not an official degree
   audit. Not affiliated with <institution>." alone. The program and institution come from the
   curriculum file (another program's file prints its own). — `print` "says what the
   paper is, at its top"; evaluator (PDF text).
5. **The settings line.** On paper, in the place of the pace controls: the target, the
   fewest, the most and Summers as the controls show them, also after the student
   changed them; not shown on the screen; not there when there is no estimate (nothing
   left; unreadable term). — `print` "prints the pace settings as a line"; evaluator.
6. **No app controls on paper.** In the print layout no button, list, switch or link is
   visible, and none of these words: "Move later", "Show courses", "Hide courses", "All",
   "Not done", "Change", "Fix it", "Check what was read", "Use another transcript",
   "Print / save PDF", "Report a mistake". — `print` "marks every control as not
   printed" (each control of the result page is inside something the stylesheet hides in
   print; the stylesheet's print rules hide those classes); evaluator (print media: no
   visible control; PDF text has none of the words).
7. **The whole result is on paper.** The PDF (made as in check 3 (a)) holds in its text: the credits left and the bar's
   numbers; the pace answer and "Credits only — course order may add semesters."; every
   requirement's name, status and courses; the sequence sentence; every semester of the
   plan with its credits and entries; "Always the final semester"; the draft label; a
   moved course in the semester it was moved to; with rows to fix, the note; with the
   legacy answer, the legacy line. — evaluator (PDF text for `sample-a`, for `sample-b`
   with Yes, and for a transcript with a row to fix).
8. **Readable on paper.** Letter and A4, portrait, the browser's default margins: nothing
   runs off the right edge; no requirement card, semester or plan entry is split across
   pages when it fits on one; no heading is the last thing on a page; the ring, bars and
   tinted status rows are visible. From a device in dark mode the paper is the same:
   in print media the computed text colour and page background are the print values
   (dark on white), not the dark-mode ones. — evaluator (PDF pages as pictures; computed
   colours in print media with a dark device); `print` "keeps paper white on a dark
   device" (the print colours come after the dark ones in the stylesheet, on a selector
   that is no weaker).
9. **The screen is unchanged** apart from the button: at 1440 and 375, light and dark, the
   result looks as before; the button matches the design's (primary, with icon, 44px
   high at least); no console error. — evaluator; `left-page` (existing tests pass).
10. **No "ask your advisor".** The only mention of an advisor is the button's own words.
    — `print` "offers print with the result".
11. **Records.** Decision log; DESIGN.md ("Print": what is and is not printed, the two
    paper-only lines); `_carried-forward.md` (F17's notes removed; for F18: the banner
    must print too, and the top line shares the footer's code; for F19: "Clear my data"
