# F15 — Flow A pace line, with a transcript only

Milestone: M1 · Branch: `feat/F15-pace-line` · Brief sections: §4 Flow A step 5

## What the student gets
On "Check my courses", once their transcript is added, the student sees one line under
the numbers: at the pace of the courses they typed, when they would finish, and how that
compares with their target semester.

## User-visible change
Yes.

## What is built
On "Check my courses" (`#/check`), under the three numbers, only when a reviewed transcript
is used for the answers (F12) and the list has at least one course:

- **One line**, with two small lists inside it, as the owner's example of 2026-11-16 has
  it: "15 credits in [Spring 2027 ▾] — at this pace you finish Fall 2029, one semester
  after your target [Spring 2029 ▾]."
- **The term** the typed courses are for: by default the next Fall or Spring after the
  latest term on the transcript (the owner's rule; F14's `latestTermOf` and `termAfter`
  with summers off). The list offers that term and the three Fall and Spring terms after
  it.
- **The target semester**: the same one as on "See what I have left" (one target for the
  visit: choosing it on either page changes it on both). Until the student chooses, it is
  F14's default (the earliest semester reachable at the most credits a term). The list
  offers what F14's list offers with summers off: Fall and Spring terms only. A summer
  target chosen on the other page (with Summers on) is shown here as itself, as the
  chosen entry of the list, and the finish is compared with it in calendar order
  (Spring, Summer, Fall): a finish in the Spring before it is "one semester before", in
  the Fall after it "one semester after"; an exact hit is not possible. No other summer
  is offered, and none is counted in the finish. The target is not changed by being
  shown. A target earlier than the chosen term is allowed: the finish is then "after" it.
- **Under the line**, always: "Credits only — course order may add semesters."
- Without a transcript, while it is still being checked, or when no row of it was taken
  on: no line, no list, no question about pace.

## How the line is worked out
- **Credits still to take** = as for F14: the credits left of the checklist plus the extra
  credits of a sequence course when both sequence picks are held.
- **Credits this term toward the degree** = the credits of the typed courses whose answer
  is "Counts toward CIS", plus those of a typed sequence course that is "Needed for the …
  sequence (3 extra credits)" (it is among the credits still to take). Courses that do
  not count, are already passed or in progress, or have their slot filled add nothing:
  they do not bring the student closer to finishing.
- **"At this pace"** = that many credits in the chosen term and in every Fall and Spring
  after it. The terms between the latest term of the transcript and a later chosen term
  are not counted. The student finishes in the term in which the credits still to take
  run out.
- **Against the target**: the number of Fall and Spring terms between the finish and the
  target.

## What the line says
| Case | Line |
|---|---|
| every typed credit counts toward the degree | "<n> credits in [term] — at this pace you finish <term>, <k> semesters after your target [target]." / "… before your target …" / "<n> credits in [term] — at this pace you finish in your target semester [target]." |
| some typed credits do not count | "<n> of your <total> credits in [term] count toward CIS — at this pace you finish …" (the pace uses <n>) |
| none of the typed credits counts | "None of these credits counts toward CIS, so this list doesn’t bring you closer to finishing." (no lists, no caveat) |
| the legacy answer is not given and a typed row shows the question | "Answer “Are you a legacy CIS student?” to see your pace." (no lists; the question is on the row) |
| the legacy answer is not given, it would change the credits still to take, and no typed row shows the question | the question itself in the line's place, with Yes and No and the reason ("Your transcript has FLS 161, which counts as an elective only for legacy students."); the same remembered answer as everywhere (F05) |
| nothing is left to take | "No credits left to plan" (F14's sentence; no lists) |
| the latest term of the transcript cannot be read | F14's sentence: "Pace can’t be estimated: the latest term on your transcript could not be read." |

"one semester" / "2 semesters", as in F14. When rows of the transcript still need fixing,
the line is shown as above, and the card's sentence under the list says so of pace too:
"<m> rows of your transcript still need fixing, so these answers are not exact yet. The
pace may change too."

**The legacy answer** can matter here with no typed course depending on it: the credits
still to take come from the checklist, where a legacy course of the transcript is an
elective only for a legacy student (with the second made-up transcript and CIS 300
typed: 89 credits with Yes, 92 with No). Until it is answered the line names no finish.

## Decided here (recorded in `docs/decision-log.md`)
- **The number in the line is the credits that count**, not every typed credit: a course
  that does not count does not bring the finish closer, and "at this pace you finish"
  with the full total would promise too much. When the two differ the line says both
  ("12 of your 15 credits in [term] count toward CIS — …"). The owner's example ("15 credits in …")
  is the case where they are the same.
- **One target semester for both flows**, kept for the visit.
- **Fall and Spring only**: the term list and the finish never use a summer (the owner:
  "Fall/Spring"; "Summer terms are skipped"). The Summers switch of "See what I have
  left" does not change this line.
- **The term list**: the default term and the next three ("the next few after it").
- **A chosen term later than the default** leaves the terms in between out of the count:
  it can only make the finish later, never promise too much.
- **A summer target** set on the other page is shown as itself and compared in calendar
  order, so the line never says "in your target semester" or "before" for a finish that
  is later than the student's own target. (Contract review, round 2: reading it as the
  Fall after it could call a late finish on target.)
- **Counting semesters against a summer target**: Fall and Spring terms, with the target
  summer as one step (a finish of Spring 2027 against Summer 2028 is "3 semesters before":
  Fall 2027, Spring 2028, Summer 2028).
- **The legacy question in the line's place** when the answer would change the credits
  still to take and no typed row shows it.
- **Read out**: a change of either list, or of the course list, says the new line once in
  the page's status line; no second live region.
- **Where it sits**: one line under the three numbers, in the agent's layout; the design
  has no place for it.
- **Wording by the agent**, listed in the pull request: "<n> of your <total> credits in
  [term] count toward CIS — …"; "at this pace you finish in your target semester"; the
  none-count sentence; the legacy sentence; "The pace may change too." on the card.

### What the student gets
On "Check my courses" with a transcript, one sentence that says how many of the typed

### What is built
- The sentence, word for word: "<n> of these <m> credits count. To finish by <target>, you
  need about <x> counting credits a term."
  - n: the typed credits that count toward the degree: the number the page itself shows
    above "Count toward CIS". A sequence course that is "Needed for the … sequence" is
    not among them (it fills the sequence requirement only and is never said to count),
    but its credits are among those still to take. (Changed after the code review of the
    change: until then n included such a course, and the line could say "3 of these 3
    credits count" under "0 Count toward CIS".) m: all the typed credits.
  - x: the credits still to take toward the degree, this term's counting courses among
    them, divided by the Fall and Spring terms from the term the typed courses are for
    through the target, both counted; rounded up. Summers are never counted here.
    The credits still to take are those of "See what I have left" (with a sequence
    course's extra credits), and 3 more when one of the typed courses fills the last
    pick a sequence could have gone through, so that a sequence course becomes necessary
    (the case F15's code review found): x is never lower than what the list really
    leaves to do.
  - The owner's words are kept exactly for every number, also for a 1 ("about 1 counting
    credits a term", "1 of these 1 credits count"); listed for the owner in the pull
    request.
- When x is more than the student's "Most credits per term" (18 until changed on "See what
  I have left"), the sentence goes on: " That's more than your <Most>-credit limit —
  consider a later target."
- Under the sentence, on a line of their own: "Term for these courses" with its list, and
  "Target semester" with its list. The names are shown beside the lists (they were only
  read out before).
- Under them, as before: "Credits only — course order may add semesters."
- The target is still the one of "See what I have left"; choosing it here changes it
  there. When that target is earlier than the term the courses are for, the line takes
  the term itself as its target (nothing is left to a target before it), and the target

- Unchanged: no line without a transcript in use or with an empty list; "No credits left

  line's place, and "Answer “Are you a legacy CIS student?” to see your pace."
