# F23 — When upload is unavailable, say so plainly; Flow A still works

Milestone: M2 · Branch: `feat/F23-upload-unavailable` · Brief sections: §7 (Limits), §4
(Flow A), §9 (Product; Safe)

## What the student gets
Whenever a transcript cannot be read for a reason that is not the student's (the reader
is down or slow, a limit is reached, the bot check does not pass or cannot run), the page
says one plain sentence where the upload control is: "Upload isn’t available right now —
try again tomorrow". Nothing of the file is taken on, and "Check my courses" goes on
working without a transcript, exactly as it does for a student who never uploads.

## User-visible change
Small. The sentence and its place exist since F11, and F21 and F22 lead every such
failure to it: this feature proves that, cause by cause and page by page, on the built
site. Two things change for the student (contract review, rounds 1 and 2): a refusal now
stands on the page the student is on when it comes, and on "See what I have left" it
goes when the student changes page, as it already did on "Check my courses".

## What is built
- **One test of the whole path, per cause and per page.** The built site's app, with
  stand-ins for the reader and for Cloudflare's widget, on "See what I have left", on
  "Check my courses", and with a file chosen on Start. The causes:
  - an outage: the reader cannot be reached; does not answer in time; answers 500, 502,
    503 or 404; answers something that is no answer (not JSON, no list of rows);
  - a limit: the day's three uploads are used (429), the month's cap is reached (429),
    the browser's number is refused (400);
  - the bot check: refused by the function (403); Cloudflare's script blocked; the widget
    failing, or nobody ticking its box in time.
- **For each of them:** the sentence stands in the message place of the page the student
  is on (from Start: on "See what I have left"), it is read out once, the reading
  sentence and the tick sentence are gone, nothing is taken on and nothing of the file
  is kept in the browser, the upload control is still there with the keyboard on it, and
  a later try with a working reader is read.
- **When the student has changed page while the file was being read** (it can take
  seconds): a refusal stands on the page the student is on when it comes: under the
  upload control of "See what I have left", or under the transcript card of "Check my
  courses". It is read out once, and the student is not moved: a refusal leaves nothing
  to look at on another page, unlike a file that was read. (Until now a refusal took the
  student to "See what I have left" unless they were still on "Check my courses" where
  they chose the file: changed here.) Only a student who is on Start, which has no place
  for the sentence and nothing in use to lose, is taken to "See what I have left".
- **The keyboard** is put on that page's upload control ("Browse files", "Choose
  transcript PDF") unless, on "Check my courses", it is in something the student is
  filling in: the course field, where it stays with what was typed, or the credits list
  of a course PathCheck doesn't know, which is drawn again with the card and gets the
  keyboard back. The status line says the sentence. ("See what I have left" has nothing
  but its upload control while no transcript is there.)
- **How long it stays, on both pages alike:** until the next file is chosen, until "Clear
  my data", or until the student goes to another page: coming back, the page does not
  show it again (F11's rule for a refusal on "Check my courses", now the same on "See
  what I have left").
- **Flow A after it:** with the sentence showing on "Check my courses", courses are added
  and answered with "unless you’ve already passed it or filled this slot", the totals
  and their notes are right, the legacy question works, the status says "No transcript
  added", and there is no pace line: the page is the one a student without a transcript
  has.
- **What the sentence does not replace:** a file that is the student's to fix keeps its
  own sentence (not a PDF; too large), also when the function is the one that says so
  (415, 413).
- **Fixes** for whatever these tests, the evaluator or the reviewer find. Each is listed
  under "QA rounds" with its test.

## Out of scope
- Telling the student which limit was reached, or how many uploads are left today.
- Another sentence for a failed bot check: the feature's step gives one sentence for an
  outage and for a limit, and the bot check standing in the way is treated as one of
  them. Listed for the owner.
- The published site and the owner's keys (F24).

## Waiting for the owner
- Nothing blocks. **For the owner to know:** "try again tomorrow" is also what a student
  reads when the cause may pass sooner (a short outage at Google, a bot check that did
  not pass). The brief's sentence is used for all of them, as the feature's step says.
- **For the owner to know:** when the student's own browser blocks Cloudflare's script
  (an ad or privacy blocker), the sentence also says "try again tomorrow", although
  tomorrow will be the same for that student. PathCheck cannot tell a blocked script
  from Cloudflare being down, so the one sentence stays; other words would be the
  owner's to give.
