§8 · Design: `docs/design/DESIGN.md` "Page 2: Check my courses" (the add field, the typing

"Credits of a course PathCheck doesn't know"

## What the student gets
On "Check my courses" the student types a course code or part of a title, picks from the
suggestions or adds what they typed, and sees their list of next semester's courses, each
with its title and credits. A course can be removed again. The answers ("does it count?")
come with the next feature, F08.

## User-visible change
Yes.

## What is built
- **The "Check my courses" page** (`#/check`), replacing the heading-only page of F06: the
  page head, the "Add a course" field with its suggestions and "Add" button, and the list
  of entered courses as the design's table with three columns: Course (code over title),
  Credits, and a Remove button. F08 adds the "Does it count?" column.
- **Suggestions come from the curriculum**: every course listed in a block, the named
  elective and the legacy-only electives, each once. A course matches by code when its
  code starts with what was typed, ignoring spaces, dashes and letter case ("mgt3",
  "MGT-3", "mgt 3"). It matches by title when the typed words, in order, each start the
  title's words at some position, ignoring letter case: "manag" and "principles of ma"
  match "Principles of Management"; "of management" does too (it starts at the second
  word); "anagement" matches nothing. Code matches come first, in curriculum order; at
  most six are shown. CIS 304 and CIS 415 are never suggested (they are not courses of the
  curriculum).
- **Adding.** When the list opens, its first suggestion is highlighted. Clicking or tapping
  a suggestion adds that course. "Add" or Enter adds: the typed text, when it is a
  complete course code ("BIO 230", "mkt410", "mgt 321"), in or out of the curriculum and
  whatever is highlighted; otherwise the highlighted suggestion; with neither, nothing,
  and a line under the field says what to type.
- **Each entry shows** its code; its title when the curriculum has one (a course outside
  the curriculum, or an elective by the rule, has no title: the design shows none); and
  its credits: the curriculum's for a listed course, the elective rule's for an elective
  by the rule, and `UNKNOWN_COURSE_CREDITS` for a course PathCheck doesn't know, CIS 304
  and CIS 415 included (changing that number is F09).
- **Removing.** Every entry has a Remove button named after its course.
- The list lives in memory for the visit: it is still there after going to Start or to
  "See what I have left" and back. Keeping it across visits is F19.
- Typed text is never put into the page as it is: only a course code worked out from it.

## Not in this feature (the page's other parts in the design)
The summary strip and the credit total (F09); the "Does it count?" column, the answers,
the two filter buttons and the sequence reminder (F08); "Add your transcript for an exact
answer" (F12). They are not drawn until their feature builds them.

## Decided here (recorded in `docs/decision-log.md`)
- **F07 is finished together with F08.** Its steps 3 ("…gets an answer") and 4 ("…before
  and after seeing answers"), and half of step 2 ("…next to every answer"), need the
  answers, which are F08. This pull request merges the entering with `passes: false` and a
  BLOCKED result (as for F05); F08's contract carries the three on-screen checks, and
  F08's pull request marks F07 passing.
- **How a course matches what was typed** (above), at most six suggestions, and what Enter
  adds.
- **The same course is not entered twice.** Typing a course already in the list adds
  nothing and says "<code> is already in your list."
- **With no courses yet**, the table is not shown; a line under the field says "Add the
  courses you plan to take next semester." This empty state is not in the design: it is
  listed in the pull request for the owner.
- **The page heading is "Check my courses"**, with no semester: the owner, 2026-11-16, "no
  semester in the heading. It says "Check my courses". Nothing is derived from today's
  date." (The contract was agreed with this as an open question; the answer came during
  QA.) The small label above the heading, which says the same in the design, is left out
  so the page does not say it twice. "No transcript added", at the right of the heading,
  comes with F12.
- **A DOM for tests.** The typing, the keyboard and the redrawing are tested in a
  simulated browser (`happy-dom`, pinned to an exact version, a development-only
  dependency that is not in what the student downloads). What a simulated browser models
  poorly (a click landing after the field loses focus, text still being composed, touch)
  is also checked by the evaluator in a real browser.

## Acceptance checks
Tests are in `tests/check.test.ts` (the rules and the page's text) and
`tests/check-page.test.ts` (typing and keyboard, in a simulated browser). "Evaluator" means
checked in a real browser at 1440 and 375 (with touch at 375), against the screenshots.

1. **Suggestions by code.** Typing "mgt 3" lists MGT 321 "Principles of Management" among
   courses whose code starts with MGT 3, in curriculum order; "mgt3", "MGT-3" and " Mgt 3 "
   give the same list. "cis" lists CIS courses, six at most. — "suggests by code, however
   it is typed".
2. **Suggestions by title.** "manag" lists courses with a title word starting with
   "manag" (among them MGT 321); "principles of ma" and "of management" match "Principles
   of Management"; "anagement" and "management of" match no title. Code matches come
   before title matches. — "suggests by title".
3. **Only the curriculum.** Every suggestion is a course listed in the curriculum, shown
   with its curriculum title; the legacy-only electives and FIN 201 can be suggested
   ("spanish" finds FLS 161); PSY 251, listed as a pick and as a legacy elective, is
   suggested once; CIS 304 and CIS 415 never are, for any text ("cis 3", "cis 304",
   "cis 4"); a course outside the curriculum ("bio 2") gets no suggestion. With a test
   curriculum, the suggestions follow that curriculum. — "suggests only courses of the
   curriculum", "never suggests the two courses that never count".
4. **Nothing typed, nothing suggested.** An empty or blank field shows no suggestions and
   "Add" is disabled; one letter does not suggest yet (two characters are needed). —
   "suggests nothing for too little text".
5. **Adding a suggested course.** With "mgt 3" typed the first suggestion is highlighted;
   Enter or "Add" adds it ("MGT" typed, then Enter, adds the first suggestion). Clicking or
   tapping any suggestion adds that one, although the
   field loses focus first: the list does not close before the click lands. The highlight
   follows the mouse. "Add" adds the highlighted suggestion also after the field has lost
   the keyboard (a tap on the button, or Tab to it and Enter): leaving the field hides the
   list but keeps the highlight (made explicit after QA round 1). A row "MGT 321 / Principles of Management / 3 cr" appears, the field
   is emptied, the suggestions close and the keyboard is in the field. Clicking outside
   the field and the list closes the list and keeps the text. — "adds a course of the
   curriculum with its title and credits"; `check-page` "adds the highlighted suggestion
   with Enter", "adds the highlighted suggestion with the Add button", "adds a suggestion on click",
   "closes the list on a click elsewhere", "moves the highlight without drawing the list
   again";
   evaluator (mouse at 1440, touch at 375).
6. **A complete code wins.** Typing a complete course code and pressing Enter or "Add" adds
   that course, whatever is highlighted: "bio 230" adds "BIO 230" with no title and the
   unknown-course credits; "mkt410" adds "MKT 410" with no title and the elective rule's
   credits; "cis 304" adds "CIS 304" with no title and the unknown-course credits (not
   the elective rule's); "cis 300" typed in full adds CIS 300 even when another course is
   highlighted. — "adds a course that is not in the curriculum", "adds the code that was
   typed in full"; `check-page` "adds what was typed when it is a whole code".
7. **What cannot be added.** "basket weaving", "ZZZ", "PSY 9", "321", "MGT 3211",
   "MGT 321, CIS 300" (several codes at once): nothing is added, the field keeps the
   text, and a line under the field (announced to a screen reader) says "Type a course
   code like MGT 321, or pick a course from the list." The line goes away at the next
   keystroke. — "refuses text that is not a course"; `check-page` "says what to type".
8. **No course twice.** Adding MGT 321 again, by suggestion or by typing "mgt-321", adds
   nothing and says "MGT 321 is already in your list."; a course already in the list is
   still shown in the suggestions. — "does not add a course twice".
9. **The title is beside every entry.** Every row of a curriculum course shows its title
   under its code; rows keep the order in which they were added. A mistyped code that is
   not in the curriculum ("MGT 312") shows no title, which is how the typo shows.
   — "shows the title next to every entry".
10. **Removing.** Each row's button is named "Remove <code>"; pressing it removes that row
    only, the others keep their order, and the keyboard moves to the next row's Remove
    button, or to the field when the list is empty. A removed course can be added again.
    — "removes one course"; `check-page` "moves the keyboard after a removal".
11. **The empty list.** With no courses the table is absent and the line "Add the courses
    you plan to take next semester." is shown; it goes when the first course is added and
    returns when the last is removed. — "shows the empty state".
12. **Keyboard and screen reader.** The field is a combobox: its label is "Add a course";
    Down and Up move the highlight through the suggestions and wrap; Enter adds; Escape
    closes the suggestions and keeps the text; Tab leaves the field and closes the list.
    The expanded state, the list and the highlighted option are exposed (`aria-expanded`,
    `aria-controls`, `aria-autocomplete="list"`, `aria-activedescendant`, a labelled
    `role="listbox"`, `role="option"` with `aria-selected` and its position, "1 of 6").
    After a course is added or removed, a visually hidden polite status says "MGT 321
    added." or "MGT 321 removed.". Typing is never interrupted: the field is not redrawn
    while typing (the caret stays where it was). — `check-page` "moves through the
    suggestions with the arrow keys", "closes on Escape", "keeps the field while the list
    redraws", "says what was added and removed"; evaluator (keyboard only; the names and
    states a screen reader gets).
13. **Phones and input methods.** The field has `autocomplete="off"`, `autocorrect="off"`,
    `autocapitalize="off"`, `spellcheck="false"` and `enterkeyhint="done"`; the phone
    keyboard's Enter key adds like Enter. While text is still being composed by an input
    method, Enter never adds and the arrows do not move the highlight. Pasting "mgt321"
    behaves like typing it. — `check-page` "does not add while text is being composed",
    "treats pasted text like typed text"; evaluator (touch emulation at 375).
14. **Long text and markup do no harm.** 500 characters, or `<img src=x onerror=alert(1)>`,
    typed or pasted: the layout holds, no element is created from the text, nothing runs,
    nothing is added, and the message of check 7 is shown. A course code is the only thing
    of the typed text that ever reaches the page. — "never puts typed text into the page",
    `check-page` "does not turn typed markup into elements"; evaluator.
15. **The list survives moving about.** Add two courses, go to Start, to "See what I have
    left", and back to "Check my courses": the two rows are there, and the field is empty.
    A reload empties the list (until F19). — `check-page` "keeps the list while the
    student moves between pages"; evaluator.
16. **It looks like the approved design.** The page head, the field with its button, the
    open suggestion menu ("mgt 3" → MGT 321 highlighted) and the rows match
    `check-typing-*` and `check-*` at 1440 and 375, in light and dark, for the parts this
    feature builds. Differences listed in the pull request: the heading text and no small
    label above it; no status at the right of the heading; no "Does it count?" column and
    the other parts left to F08, F09 and F12; the empty-state line; the two message lines
    under the field. At 375 nothing is hidden or overlapping and the suggestion menu stays
    inside the screen. Contrast meets WCAG AA; focus is visible on the field, the button,
    every option and every Remove button. — evaluator.
17. **Nothing is said about counting.** There is no "Does it count?" column, and no
    "Counts", "Doesn't count" or total anywhere on the page. — "says nothing yet about
    what counts"; evaluator.
18. **No curriculum fact in the app's source**, and nothing leaves the browser: the scans
    of F06 still pass with the new files; the built page makes no request after loading
    other than for its own code-font file when the first course code is shown (reworded in
    QA: nothing of the student's is sent, and nothing goes to another origin); the build
    output does not contain the test-only dependency. — the scans in
    `tests/curriculum.test.ts`, `tests/start.test.ts` "does not read a dropped file";
    evaluator.
19. **F07 is not marked passing.** In `features.json`, F07 has `passes: false`, its tests
    named, and evidence that says steps 3 and 4 wait for F08; this file's "Result" starts
    with BLOCKED; `node scripts/status.mjs` names F08 as the next feature.
    `docs/sprints/_carried-forward.md` has, for F08: the three on-screen checks that
    finish F07 (a course outside the curriculum gets its answer; courses can be added and
    removed before and after answers are shown; every answer row shows the course title
    under its code), how the list is held and passed on (no course twice; typed order),
    and that the "Does it count?" column and the row tint are F08's.
    `docs/decision-log.md` has the "Decided here" entries, a row of its own for the
    test-only dependency with its exact version, and the open question about the heading.
    — the files; the command; reviewer.
