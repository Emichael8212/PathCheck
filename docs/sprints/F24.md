# F24 — Real upload on a staging address on Netlify's free plan

Milestone: M2 (its last feature) · Branch: `feat/F24-staging` · Brief sections: §7
(Hosting; Tests and real data), §9 (Correct: "The transcript reader passes on synthetic

## What the student gets
Nothing new is built for the student. This feature puts what F21 to F23 built on the
staging address, `https://pathcheck.netlify.app`, with the owner's own keys, shows that
both flows work there, and ends with the owner uploading their own transcript and
checking the rows read from it. After it the owner checks M2.

## User-visible change
None of the agent's making. What the owner's real transcript shows may ask for changes
(how a summer term or a transfer row is printed, a grade the curriculum's scale does not
have): each is put to the owner as a question, and none is guessed at. An academic rule is
never invented.

## Who does what
The project is Private on Netlify and the agent may not send a file to an outside address
(the repository's guard), may not read or set a secret, and may not publish. So on the
staging address **the owner does every step**, with the agent's exact clicks, and the
agent and the evaluator judge from what the owner reports: screenshots and words. The
agent's part is the instructions, the record, and fixing what the steps find.

     `GEMINI_API_KEY` (secret).
  3. A new build of `main` with those variables ("Trigger deploy"), and, because

7. **The owner's own transcript (the feature's step 4): the stop.** The owner uploads their own
   unofficial transcript PDF on the staging address and compares "Check what was read"
   with the PDF, row by row: every course on the PDF is a row, in its term, with its
   code, credits and grade; no row that is not on the PDF; the lines that are not courses
   (GPA, standing, totals) are not rows; transfer credit is labelled as such; rows that
   need fixing say why. Then "Continue": the result has no label "Synthetic transcript
   result", and the credits left and the requirements are what the owner expects. The
   owner reports what differs, in words: **nothing of the real transcript is sent to the
   agent, pasted in chat or committed** (no screenshot of it is asked for; if the owner
   sends one, it is not copied into the repository). Evidence: the owner's words only.
   - **The reader passes when** every course on the PDF is one row in its term, with its
     code, credits, transfer mark and grade as printed; there is no row that is not on
     the PDF, and no totals line is a row.
   - A row the reader could not read with certainty may be a row to fix, if it says why.
     A row read **wrongly** and shown as good (a wrong code, credits or grade) is a
     failure.
   - **A failure blocks F24** (brief §9: the reader passes "on the owner's real one"). It
     is fixed here (the instruction to Gemini, the reader's checks), with a new build
     and the owner's publish, and the owner uploads again, until it passes.
   - Only an academic-rule question (for example whether a "P" counts) does not block:
     it is listed for the owner, and the answer comes as its own change. — owner.
8. **What the real transcript asks.** Each of the questions open since M1 is answered by
   the owner from their own PDF, or stays open and listed: how a summer term is printed
   (and a winter or mini-term); what the grade column of a transfer row shows; how the
   transfer section and its totals line are printed; whether a grade such as "P" or
   "Passed" appears, and if so whether it counts (an academic rule: the owner's). A

   rule and small; otherwise it is listed for the owner with what it would take. — owner;
   the record.
9. **On a phone.** The owner repeats check 3 on their phone (the file picker with a real
   PDF, open since M1): it works, or what happens is recorded. Evidence: the phone's

   computer's.) — owner.
10. **Records.** `docs/milestones/M2.md` as above; the decision log (the owner's answers of
