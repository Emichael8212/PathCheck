# F20 — Both flows end to end on phone and desktop, built to match the mockup the owner picked

Milestone: M1 · Branch: `feat/F20-both-flows` · Brief sections: §4 (both flows), §8, §2

## What the student gets
Nothing new on the page. This is the check that the two flows, built one feature at a
time, work together from the first click to the last, on a phone and on a desktop, as
the brief describes them and as the owner's design shows them. After it the owner checks.

## User-visible change
Yes, small: whatever the walk-through finds is fixed here, and one known flaw is fixed
(the focus ring of "Change" touched the "Yes" beside it in "Legacy CIS student: Yes
Change").

## What is built
- **One end-to-end test of each flow**, written from brief §4 step by step, in the
  simulated browser, so that the whole path is run on every change, not only its parts.
- **The fixes** for anything the walk-through, the evaluator or the reviewer finds in the
  flows as a whole. Each is listed in "QA rounds" with its test.
- **The record of the M1 check**, `docs/milestones/M1.md`, linked from "Milestone checks" in
  `docs/decision-log.md` and marked as waiting for the owner. Its parts:
  1. how to open the site on a desktop, and on a phone (the development server on the
     home network with one command, and the address to type on the phone; or the desktop
     browser's phone view), and how to get the two made-up PDFs onto the phone; that a
     real transcript is refused at M1;
  2. three to five steps for each flow, each with what the owner should see ("sample-a →
     81 credits left, On pace for Spring 2029");
  3. every open item, gathered from all pull requests of M1: the agent's wording and
     layout choices awaiting the owner, each "conflict with the brief", the questions
     without an answer (the review-step screen of F11; the PSY 251 legacy question of
     F13), what could not be checked on this machine (a real iPhone file picker, Safari
     and Firefox, a physical printer), the placeholder contact address;
  4. every merged pull request of M1 with its feature, and the result of the evaluator's
     and the reviewer's last round for each.
- **Two commands for the check**: `npm run dev:lan` (the development server reachable from
  a phone on the same network) and `npm run preview` (the built site, where every file
  is answered "Upload isn’t available right now — try again tomorrow").
- Nothing else: no new feature, no new wording, no academic rule.

## Acceptance checks
Tests: `tests/flows.test.ts` (`flows`: both flows from Start, in the simulated browser),
`tests/mockups.test.js` (existing: the tokens, their light, dark and print values, and
their contrast). "Evaluator": a real browser on the development server as a first-time
student, at 1440 and 375 (and 320), light and dark, with the made-up transcripts only.

1. **Start.** Two big choices and nothing to fill in first; each opens its flow. — `flows`
   "starts with two choices"; evaluator.
2. **Flow A, step by step (brief §4).**
   - Courses are entered by code or by title; suggestions come from the curriculum; the
     title is beside every answer.
   - One answer per course, in the brief's words: "Counts toward CIS — <block>" with
     "unless you’ve already passed it or filled this slot" when there is no transcript;
     "Doesn’t count toward CIS — not in the CIS curriculum".
   - The credit total, with the neutral note under 12 ("below full-time; may affect
     scholarships") and over 18 ("may cost extra"), and none between.
   - "Add your transcript for an exact answer": the transcript is chosen from this page,
     reviewed, and the student is back here; then, with a transcript only, "You already
     passed this" and "This slot is already filled", and no "unless …".
   - Pace, with a transcript only: the target semester is asked and one line says when
     the student finishes at this pace, with "Credits only — course order may add
     semesters."
   — `flows` "walks Flow A as the brief has it"; evaluator (mouse and keyboard).
3. **Flow B, step by step (brief §4).**
   - The transcript is the only input: the page has no field to type a course in.
   - The review step shows what was read; a misread row is fixed there.
   - In progress: shown "In progress", its requirement not Done, the course not in the
     plan. Below C, W and I: not counted. Transfer, AP and CLEP rows count when shown
     under a course code; with no grade shown no grade check is applied (`sample-b`'s
     rows from the transfer section, with and without a grade).
   - The checklist: each requirement Done, In progress or Still needed.
   - Pace: the target semester, the range (12 to 18 to start with, changeable), Summers
     (off to start with), the answer, and "Credits only — course order may add
     semesters."
   - The draft plan: by level, MGT 462 last, each semester within the range, "Order not
     checked — your portal checks prerequisites", "Move later" on each entry and the plan
     flowing again; nothing to adopt.
   - "Print / save PDF for your advisor" opens the browser's print.
   — `flows` "walks Flow B as the brief has it"; evaluator (also the PDF).
4. **The two flows together.** A transcript brought in one flow is used in the other; the
   legacy answer and the target semester are the same in both; after a reload both are
   as they were left; "Clear my data" empties both. — `flows` "keeps the two flows
   together"; evaluator.
13. **If upload is unavailable (brief §4).** On the built site (`npm run build`, then
    `npm run preview`), choosing any PDF on "See what I have left" or on "Check my courses"
    shows exactly "Upload isn’t available right now — try again tomorrow", and "Check my
    courses" still answers typed courses, with "unless you’ve already passed it or filled
    this slot". On the development server a file that is not one of the two made-up
    PDFs is refused with its own sentence, and Flow A works as well. — `flows` "still
    checks courses when upload is unavailable"; evaluator (on the preview address).
5. **Within 10 seconds.** A first-time student opens the site and has the answer for one
   course within 10 seconds, with no instructions: from Start, one choice, a course typed,
   Enter. The page asks nothing first and explains nothing first. — `flows` "answers a
   first course in three steps" (one click, typing, Enter: no more is needed, and the
   keyboard alone does it too); evaluator, timed:
   - the clock starts when the Start page is requested, on a fresh load with nothing
     kept;
   - about 1 second to read Start and press "Check my courses"; "MGT 321" typed at 5
     characters a second (a 40-words-a-minute typist); Enter; no suggestion picked by
     mouse;
   - the clock stops when that course's row shows its answer ("Counts toward CIS —
     Business core") on screen without the student scrolling: the answer must be in
     view, and if the page has to scroll it scrolls to the answer itself;
   - three runs at 1440 and three at 375, light; every run under 10 seconds;
   - "with no instructions" is judged as a first-time student: Start makes the choice
     obvious, and nothing has to be read before typing.
6. **Matches the owner's design.** At desktop and phone width, light and dark; no
   difference beyond those listed in `docs/design/DESIGN.md` ("Where the app differs from
   the screenshots"); none of the banned elements of brief §8 beyond what the owner
   approved (evaluator's Calibration). — evaluator, state by state:

   | Screenshots (`docs/design/screens/`) | The app's state that reproduces them |
   |---|---|
   | `start-*`, `start-demo-step2…4-*` | Start, and the demo at its second, third and fourth frame |
   | `check-*` | "Check my courses" with the design's list (the heading without a semester, as listed in DESIGN.md) |
   | `check-typing-*` | the same page with text in the field and the suggestions open |
   | `left-*` | the result for `sample-a`, as it opens |
   | `left-open-*` | the same with the requirements opened as the screenshot has them |
   | `left-notes-*` | the same at the requirements with notes (the repeated course, the open picks) |

   States with no screenshot, graded against the design's own elements and DESIGN.md's
   listed differences: the review step (with a row to fix and a row being edited); a
   refused file and the "unavailable" sentence; the legacy question in both places and
   the legacy line; the rows-to-fix notes; the pace line of "Check my courses"; a plan
   that ends late; the print layout; the banner; the cleared note and "Clear my data" in
   its three places.
7. **The basics.** Every colour is a token with light, dark and print values; text and
   controls meet WCAG AA contrast in light and dark, on every state the two flows pass
   through (every state of check 6's table and list); at 375 and 320 nothing in those
   states is hidden, cut or overlapping and nothing scrolls sideways; every control that
   appears in them is at least 44px to touch. — `mockups` "defines every color as
   a token with light, dark and print values", "meets WCAG AA contrast in light and dark";
   `flows` "uses no colour outside the tokens" (the app's stylesheet); evaluator
   (measured through both flows).
8. **No console errors.** Through both flows, with every state of checks 2 to 4, at both
   widths and in both themes: no error and no warning in the browser's console (the
   page's own or the browser's about the page), and no failed request, the site's icon
   included. A warning that cannot be removed is listed with its reason, not passed
   over. — `flows` "says nothing to the console"; evaluator.
9. **The known flaw.** At 1440 and at 375 the focus ring of "Change" in "Legacy CIS
   student: Yes Change" no longer touches "Yes", on both pages that show the line. —
   evaluator.
10. **Nothing said that should not be.** Through both flows: no "ask your advisor"; every
    "Counts" without a transcript has its condition; "Not an official degree audit." and
    "Curriculum last reviewed" on every page; a finish never without its caveat; "Report a
    mistake" opens a filled-in email on every page; with the day moved past 2027-11-15
    the banner starts every page. — `flows` "keeps its promises through both flows";
    evaluator.
11. **Records.** `docs/milestones/M1.md` has its four parts (how to open it on a desktop and
    on a phone with the made-up PDFs; the steps of each flow with what to see; every open
    item; every merged pull request of M1 with the last QA and review result), each
    verifiable against the repository and GitHub; "Milestone checks" in
    `docs/decision-log.md` links to it, waiting for the owner;
    `docs/sprints/_carried-forward.md` has nothing left for M1; `features.json` F20.
12. `npm run check` passes; after the merge, `main` passes CI on the merged commit, so
    that the owner checks exactly what was graded.
