# F18 — On every result: last reviewed date, not an official audit, report a mistake, and the 12-month banner

Milestone: M1 · Branch: `feat/F18-every-result` · Brief sections: §4 "On every result", §2, §12

## What the student gets
Whatever PathCheck tells a student, it also tells them how far to trust it: that it is not
an official audit, when the curriculum behind it was last reviewed, and, once that review
is more than 12 months old, a banner that says so. One link lets them report a mistake by
email with what PathCheck showed already written into the message.

## User-visible change
Yes.

## What is built
- **The two lines** (step 1). Already on every page since F06 and the owner's review date
  (2026-11-15): "Not an official degree audit." in the top bar and the footer, and
  "Curriculum last reviewed: Nov 2026" in the footer; on paper also at the top (F17). F18
  adds nothing to them: it proves them on every result (the answers of "Check my
  courses" with and without a transcript; the checklist, the pace and the plan of "See
  what I have left"; the printed summary).
- **"Report a mistake"** (step 2): the footer's link opens the student's own mail app with
  a message to PathCheck's contact address, already filled in:
  - the subject names the page ("PathCheck: Check my courses", "PathCheck: See what I
    have left"), as today;
  - the body starts with a line for the student ("What looks wrong?") and then says what
    PathCheck showed at the moment the link is used:
    - on "Check my courses": each course of the list with the answer it has on the page
      (the answer's own words, with its condition), whether a transcript is in use, the
      legacy answer when one was given, and, with a transcript, the pace line as the
      page has it (or the sentence in its place) with its caveat "Credits only — course
      order may add semesters.", and the page's note that the answers are not exact yet
      while rows of the transcript still need fixing;
    - on "See what I have left", with the result: the page's note about rows that still
      need fixing, when there are any; the credits left, counted, in progress and in
      total; the pace answer with its caveat; the pace answer and the settings it was made with (the
      line the paper has: "Target semester: … Fewest … Most … Summers: …"); each
      requirement with its status and credits; the sequence sentence when the page
      shows one; the plan's "This draft ends in …" line when the page shows it; the
      legacy answer when one was given;
    - on every page: the program and "Curriculum last reviewed: Nov 2026";
  - nothing else: no name (PathCheck has none), no file name, no transcript row, no
    grade. PathCheck sends nothing: the message is the student's to read, change and
    send. A long list is cut so that the link stays short enough for mail apps, and says
    how many courses were left out; the link never passes 1,900 characters. It is
    filled in as soon as the link is pressed or reached (also a middle click, the link's
    own menu, the keyboard), not only when it is followed. (The caveat, the two notes
    about rows to fix and this last point were added after the code review and QA.)
  - The address is the placeholder `report@pathcheck.example` until the owner gives the
    real one before M3 (brief §12; the M3 feature "uses the contact email the owner
    provided"). Nothing is sent there at M1.
- **The banner** (step 3): when the curriculum's last review is more than 12 months before
  today, every page starts with "The curriculum PathCheck uses was last reviewed in Nov
  2026, more than 12 months ago. Requirements may have changed since: check your portal
  before you rely on this." It is the first thing on the page and the first thing on the
  printed summary. It is ordinary text that a screen reader reads first: a note with a
  name ("Curriculum may be out of date"), before the top bar in reading order, not a
  live region (it is not announced again when something on the page changes), and with
  nothing in it to press. Not otherwise: no banner on the anniversary day itself or before it,
  with a review date in the future, or when the curriculum file has no review date (the
  line is left out then, as before, and nothing is claimed).
- **No "ask your advisor"** (step 4): nothing PathCheck shows, says to a screen reader,
  prints or writes into the email sends the student to an advisor. The one mention of an
  advisor is the design's button "Print / save PDF for your advisor".

## Decided here (recorded in `docs/decision-log.md`)
- **The lines stay where the owner's design has them** (top bar and footer); "every result
  shows" them because every page does.
- **What "pre-filled with the course or result" holds**: the list above. The result's own
  words, taken from the same code that draws the page, at the moment the link is used.
- **More than 12 months** means: today is after the same day of the month twelve months
  later (reviewed 2026-11-15 → the banner from 2027-11-16; a review on the 29th, 30th or
  31st of a month that the later month does not have counts from that month's last day).
  "Today" is the day on the student's device.
- **The banner is on every page**, Start included: a student should know before checking
  a course, not only after.
- **No review date → no banner** (and no last-reviewed line, as since F06).
- **The banner is the only thing that reads today's date.** The owner's "nothing is
  derived from today's date" (given for the heading and the term of "Check my courses")
  stays true everywhere else: no other word on any page, and nothing in the email,
  changes with the day.
- **The plan's semesters are not written into the email**: the settings line and the
  "ends in" line say what produced the plan, and the link must stay short.
- **The contact address stays the placeholder** until the owner gives the real one (§12).
- **"Report a mistake" is still not printed** (F17): a link does nothing on paper, and the
  placeholder address would mislead on it.
- **Wording by the agent**, listed in the pull request: the banner's sentence; the
  email's "What looks wrong?", "What PathCheck showed:" and its lines' labels.

## Acceptance checks
Tests: `tests/trust.test.ts` (`trust`: the pages as text and the simulated browser, with
the day set by the test), `tests/start.test.ts` (the footer, existing). "Evaluator": a real
browser on the development server, 1440 and 375, light and dark, with the device's date
moved forward for the banner.

1. **The two lines on every result.** "Not an official degree audit." and "Curriculum last
   reviewed: Nov 2026" are on the page for: an answer of each kind on "Check my courses"
   (counts, doesn't count, the legacy question, needed for the sequence), with and
   without a transcript; the result of "See what I have left" (checklist, pace, plan),
   also with rows to fix and before the legacy answer; the review step; Start. On paper
   they are at the top and in the footer. — `trust` "carries the two lines on every
   result"; evaluator.
2. **The link.** "Report a mistake" is an email link (`mailto:`) to the contact address,
   on every page, with the page's subject. — `trust` "opens an email to the contact
   address"; evaluator (the link's address read, not followed).
3. **Pre-filled with the course.** On "Check my courses" with MGT 321, BIO 230 and a course
   that asks the legacy question: the body holds each code with its answer as the page
   has it ("MGT 321 — Counts toward CIS …", "BIO 230 — Doesn't count toward CIS — not in
   the CIS curriculum", the legacy question as asked); after the list changes (a course
   added, removed, the legacy question answered, a transcript added) the link used next
   holds the new list and answers. With a transcript the body holds the pace line as
   the page has it, and after the term or the target is changed the link used next
   holds the new one. With an empty list the body says no course is in the list. — `trust` "fills the email with the courses and their answers".
4. **Pre-filled with the result.** On "See what I have left" for `sample-a`: "81 credits
   left, 27 counted, 13 in progress, 121 in total", "On pace for Spring 2029", every
   requirement with status and credits ("Orientation — Done, 1 / 1 cr"), the sequence
   sentence, the settings line ("Target semester: Spring 2029. Fewest credits per term:
   12 credits. …"); after the pace settings change, the pace answer and the settings
   line in the link used next are the new ones; after a move that makes the plan end
   later, the body holds "This draft ends in …". Before a result (upload, review, the legacy question, no rows) the body
   has no result lines. — `trust` "fills the email with the result".
5. **Nothing private, nothing sent.** The body never holds the transcript's file name, a
   grade, a term of a row or the made-up descriptions; no request is made when the link
   is used; the whole link is at most 1,900 characters however long the list (30 courses
   typed: the list is cut and says how many were left out); it decodes back to the text
   it was made from. — `trust` "keeps the email short and free of transcript rows".
6. **The banner appears after 12 months.** With today 2027-11-16 and later: the banner is
   the first thing on Start, "Check my courses" and "See what I have left" (in every
   state), with the month and year of the review, and it is on the printed summary
   (not hidden in print). It is a note named "Curriculum may be out of date", before the
   top bar, with no live region and nothing focusable in it; moving between pages and
   changing the list does not announce it again. — `trust` "shows the banner once the
   review is more than 12 months old", "reads the banner first and only once";
   evaluator (device date moved; light and dark; 375; print media; the accessibility
   tree).
7. **And not otherwise.** No banner with today 2026-11-16, 2027-11-14, 2027-11-15 (the
   anniversary), before the review date, or with a curriculum without a date; a review
   on 2024-02-29 gives it from 2025-03-01, one on 2026-08-31 from 2027-09-01, one on
   2026-03-31 from 2027-04-01; the day is the device's own (no time-zone shift at
   midnight). — `trust` "shows no banner otherwise", "counts twelve months by the
   calendar".
12. **Only the banner reads today's date.** With the day set to 2020-03-15 and to
    2035-03-15, Start, "Check my courses" (without and with a transcript and its pace
    line) and "See what I have left" (with pace and plan), and each page's email body,
    are identical to each other apart from the banner (absent in 2020, present in
    2035). — `trust` "changes nothing else with the day".
8. **No "ask your advisor".** Across the pages and states of checks 1 and 6, the email
   bodies, what is said to a screen reader, and every sentence in `src/` the only
   "advisor" is the print button's; "ask / see / contact / talk to your advisor" is
   nowhere. — `trust` "never sends the student to an advisor".
9. **Looks right.** The banner at 1440 and 375, light and dark: readable (contrast AA),
   not overlapping the top bar, no horizontal scroll, the page below unchanged; without
   the banner every page is as before. No console error. — evaluator.
10. **Records.** Decision log; DESIGN.md (the banner: the design has no place for it; the
    email's content); `_carried-forward.md` (F18's notes removed; for F19: what
    "Clear my data" need not touch; for M3: the real address replaces the placeholder
    in one place); `features.json` F18.
11. `npm run check` passes.
