# F13 — Flow B degree checklist: each requirement block is Done, In progress or Still needed

Milestone: M1 · Branch: `feat/F13-checklist` · Brief sections: §4 Flow B step 3, §5

## What the student gets
After checking what was read from their transcript, the student sees how many credits are
left and, for each requirement of the degree, whether it is Done, In progress or Still
needed, with the courses still to take shown straight away.

## User-visible change
Yes. This feature also finishes F11 (step 5: continuing uses the reviewed rows) and F05
(the legacy question on the last screen that needs it).

## What is built
The result of "See what I have left" (`#/left` after "Continue"), from the approved design
of page 3, as far as the checklist goes:

- **Heading**: the label "What I have left", the heading "Your degree, in focus." and the
  pill "Synthetic transcript result" (true at M1: only the made-up transcripts can be read).
- **Credits**: the ring with the credits left in its middle, and the credit bar with its
  parts labelled where they are: "<n> counted", "<n> in progress · <term>", "<n> left",
  "<n> total". The numbers are `placeCourses`'s (credits from the curriculum, never from a
  row). The pace answer and its controls beside and under them are F14: not drawn yet.
- **Requirements**: the label "The rest of your degree", the heading "Requirements", the
  "All / Not done" toggle, the line "Credits counted / needed.", and one card for each of
  the eight blocks and for the CIS electives, in the curriculum's order. Each card: name,
  status mark and word, the segmented bar, "<counted> / <needed> cr", and "Show courses" /
  "Hide courses". Open, a card lists its courses under "Done", "In progress" and "Still
  needed".
- **Status of a requirement**: Done when every credit of it is done; otherwise In progress
  when a course in progress fills a place of it; otherwise Still needed. "Counted" is
  credits done; a course in progress never counts as done.
- **Under the list**: what the sequence still needs (below), and the courses of the
  transcript that fill nothing, each with its reason.
- **Above the result**, when it applies: how many rows still need fixing; the legacy
  question.
- At the end: "Check what was read" (back to the review, corrections kept) and "Use another
  transcript" with its note, as today.

Not drawn in this feature: pace (F14), the draft plan (F16), "Print / save PDF" (F17),
"Clear my data" (F19).

## What a card lists
| In the block | Shown |
|---|---|
| a required course that is passed | under Done: code and title |
| a required course in progress | under In progress: code and title; when an earlier attempt was below C, the note "Repeat; the earlier D doesn’t count." (the design's sentence, with the grade read) |
| a required course not passed and not in progress | under Still needed: code and title; when its best attempt was below C, the note "Must be repeated; the D doesn’t count." |
| a pick with a place open | under Still needed: "1 of ENG 131 / ENG 140" (the number still open, the courses that can still fill it); when one of them was attempted below C, the note "<code>: the D doesn’t count. It must be repeated, or another course of this pick taken." |
| a course that fills a pick | under Done or In progress, like a required course |
| the electives with places open | under Still needed: "<n> courses: FIN 201, or any 300/400-level MGT, FIN, CIS, MKT, ACT or ECO course not already required", built from the curriculum's rule ("1 course: …" for one); when an elective was attempted below C, the note "<code>: the D doesn’t count. It must be repeated, or another elective taken." |
| a course that fills an elective place | under Done or In progress: code, and its title when the curriculum has one |

W and I are not flagged: the course is simply still needed (brief §5: "W and I don't
count"; the flag is for a grade below C).

## The sequence, under the list
"Sequence course" means HIS 132 or ENG 210 (the course of each sequence that sits in a
pick; the other one is required anyway).

| The transcript… | Shown |
|---|---|
| has a sequence course passed or in progress | nothing |
| has neither, and the picks of both are open | "Still needed: HIS 132 or ENG 210 to complete a history or literature sequence." (F13 step 5) |
| has neither, and one pick is open, the other held by another course | the same sentence for the one that can still go through its pick: "Still needed: ENG 210 to complete a literature sequence." (follows the owner's answer 2a of 2026-11-16: take the course whose pick is open) |
| has neither, and both picks are held by other courses | "Needed for the history or literature sequence (3 extra credits): HIS 132 or ENG 210." (the owner's answer 2b: "Flow B shows the same") and, under the credit bar, "Plus 3 extra credits for the history or literature sequence." |

In the last case the ring and the bar still show the credits left of the degree's total:
the extra credits fill no place of it, and are said beside it. The degree is never shown
as complete while the sequence is open.

## Decided here (recorded in `docs/decision-log.md`)
- **The legacy question comes before the result.** When the answer would change anything
  of the result (the placement differs between Yes and No), the page shows the heading,
  the question with why it is asked ("Your transcript has FLS 161, which counts only for
  legacy students."), and nothing of the result until it is answered. Afterwards a line
  "Legacy CIS student: Yes" with "Change" stays above the result. The answer is the one
  remembered for "Check my courses" (one answer for both flows). When the answer changes
  nothing, nothing is asked.
- **Rows that still need fixing are said above the result**: "<m> rows of your transcript
  still need fixing and count nothing. A requirement, or the sequence, may show as still
  needed because of them." with "Fix them", which reopens the review. A "Done" is not
  affected: unchecked rows could only add to it.
- **Courses that fill nothing are listed** under the requirements, closed until asked
  for (brief §8): one line with their number ("2 courses on your transcript are not
  counted toward CIS") and "Show courses" / "Hide courses" like the cards'; opened, each
  passed or in-progress course that holds no place, with its reason in the sentences
  already used (not in the curriculum; not in the current curriculum; legacy students
  only; its pick is already filled by <course>; the elective places are already filled).
  The design has no place for them; hiding them entirely would leave a student wondering
  where a passed course went.
- **The pill "Synthetic transcript result"** is true only while the made-up transcripts
  are the only ones read: carried to F21, where it changes or goes.
- **The sequence lines for one open pick and for no open pick** apply the owner's answer of
  2026-11-16 to Flow B; the wording of the Flow B lines is the agent's reading of it and is
  listed in the pull request.
- **The in-progress label names the term** of the rows that have no grade ("13 in progress
  · Fall 2026"); several terms are joined with "and".
- **"All / Not done"** hides the Done cards; it does not redraw the page, so an opened
  card stays as the student left it.
- **"Check what was read" is always offered** on the result, not only when rows need
  fixing: a misread row may be noticed only when the checklist looks wrong.
- **Wording by the agent**, listed in the pull request: the three notes about a grade
  below C (a still-needed course, a pick, an elective); the rows-to-fix sentence; the heading and
  reasons of the not-counted list; the line under the credit bar; the two extra sequence
  lines.

## Change before the owner's own upload (owner, 2026-11-17) — branch `fix/F13-synthetic-pill`

The owner: "Show "Synthetic transcript result" only for the made-up transcripts in
synthetic/transcripts/. A real transcript's result never shows it. Add a test for both
cases. Settle this before I upload my own transcript." This section is the contract of
that change.

### What the student gets
The label "Synthetic transcript result" stands over a result only when the transcript is
one of PathCheck's made-up ones. Over a student's own transcript it never appears.

### What is built
- A transcript is known as made-up only when PathCheck itself recognised the file as one
  of those in `synthetic/transcripts/`, by its bytes. That happens on a development
  server only: the built site holds nothing of the made-up transcripts (the build check),
  so there every file is a student's own as far as PathCheck knows, and the label never
  shows, also when one of the made-up PDFs is uploaded there.
- The label is shown on the result exactly for such a transcript; it stays through the
  review, corrections and a return visit (it is kept with the result).
- On the built site a kept result is never called made-up, whatever the browser's storage
  says.
- Nothing else of the result changes.

### Acceptance checks

transcript" (both cases, as the owner asked); `tests/upload.test.ts`,
`tests/remember.test.ts`, `tests/checklist.test.ts` (existing, adjusted).

1. **A made-up transcript.** `sample-a.pdf` chosen on a development server: its result
   says "Synthetic transcript result"; still after a row is left out and "Continue" is

2. **A student's own transcript.** On the built site, a PDF read by the reader (the same
