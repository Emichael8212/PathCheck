# F01 — The owner's design as the M0 mockups

Milestone: M0 · Branch: `feat/F01-mockups` (draft PR #4) · Spec: the owner's decision of
2026-11-15 in `docs/decision-log.md` ("The owner's own design replaces the three
directions"), `docs/design/` (screenshots and DESIGN.md), `docs/M0-DESIGN-BRIEF.md` for the
fixed content, `docs/BRIEF.md` §3–§8.

**Round 3 of this feature.** Round 1 (Worksheet, Credit map, Plain answers) and round 2
(Receipt, Meter, Signs) both passed QA, and the owner approved none of them. The owner then
designed PathCheck themselves and published it. That design is the chosen direction. The
round-2 directions are deleted. This contract covers rebuilding the owner's design as the
mockups.

*Order of work this round:* the owner's message gave the steps directly, and the mockups
were built from the published design before this contract was reviewed. The contract was
then reviewed and the build checked against it. Nothing was graded before the review.

## What the student gets
Nothing yet. The owner gets their own design as three static pages in the project's code,
to approve side by side with the published design.

## User-visible change
Yes — static mockups under `mockups/`, opened from
http://127.0.0.1:5173/mockups/index.html: `start.html`, `check.html`, `check-typing.html`

behavior: the major dropdown (one option), the add-course field, Add, the two filters,
Remove, "Choose transcript PDF", the three pace controls, "All / Not done", "Move later",
"Clear my data".

## What differs from the published design, on purpose
Only what the owner's message says to keep from the brief:

1. **Demo steps 2 and 3:** under "Counts toward CIS — Business core" the mockup adds "unless
   you've already passed it or filled this slot" (brief §4 wording; the published demo shows
   the answer without it).
2. **Demo step 4:** under "On pace for Spring 2029" the mockup adds "Credits only — course
   order may add semesters." (brief §4).
3. **"Report a mistake"** is a `mailto:` link to a placeholder address with the page named
   in the subject (brief §4); the published design links to `#report`.
4. **Dark mode** follows the device setting instead of the review site's Light/Dark buttons.
5. The nav items and the two entry buttons are links, so the pages connect.

Everything else is the owner's design, unchanged, including the parts listed next.

## Conflicts with the brief: kept as designed, listed for the owner
The owner's message says not to change the design silently and to list each remaining
conflict in the PR under "Conflicts with the brief". These are kept exactly as designed:

a. **"Your major" dropdown** on Start (brief §3: CIS only, no multi-major UI; §4: nothing
   to fill in first).
b. **"12 Count toward CIS"** in the page-2 summary, in green, with no condition beside it
   (priority 1: never say a course counts when it may not). Each row below it does carry
   the condition.
c. **"Doesn't count / unconfirmed" filter**, and in the published design a third answer,
   "Can't confirm — this course isn't in PathCheck yet. Treat it as not counting." (brief
   §4: such a course gets "Doesn't count toward CIS — not in the CIS curriculum"). The
   static mockup shows only the filter label.
d. **Requirements are closed until opened, and Done requirements cannot be opened** (owner
   answer on F13, 2026-11-15: In progress and Still needed blocks show their remaining
   courses directly; Done blocks are collapsed and can be opened).
e. **Only "Most credits per term"** can be changed (brief §4: a credit range, default 12–18,
   editable).
f. **Brief §8 banned list**, beyond the four items the owner approved: an uppercase grey
   table header on page 2 at desktop width; a legend under the credit bar on page 3;
   pill-shaped tabs (the nav, "All / Not done"); an indigo-blue primary button.
g. **"Counts" and "Done" use the same mark** (a disc with a tick). The owner's M0 brief asks
   for a distinct non-color cue for each of the five statuses.
h. **On a phone the two nav links are not shown** (brief §8: at 375px nothing is hidden).
   Both pages are still reachable from Start through the wordmark.

## Acceptance checks
Automated checks run with `npm test` (`tests/mockups.test.js`). "Browser" is the evaluator
with Playwright at 1440×900 and 375×667, light and dark (`prefers-color-scheme`), comparing
against `docs/design/screens/`.

1. **Reference exists.** The 12 required screenshots are in `docs/design/screens/` at 1440
   and 375 wide; `docs/design/DESIGN.md` has the sections the owner asked for and its color
   tokens equal the ones the mockups use. No real student name, ID or grades appear in any
   screenshot or page. — test, browser (looks at the screenshots).
2. **Pages.** `mockups/index.html` links the four pages; every page links to Start, "Check
   my courses" and "What I have left"; the three round-2 directions and their files are
   gone. — test, browser.
3. **Matches the owner's design: Start.** At 1440 and 375, light and dark, `start.html`
   matches `start-*.png`: same order, layout, type, colors, spacing and marks. — browser.
4. **Matches the owner's design: Check my courses.** `check.html` matches `check-*.png` and
   `check-typing.html` matches `check-typing-*.png`, at both widths, light and dark.
   — browser.
5. **Matches the owner's design: What I have left.** `left.html` matches `left-*.png`, and
   with every requirement opened matches `left-open-*.png`, at both widths, light and dark.
   — browser.
6. **The demo.** Four frames with the published captions ("1/4 …" to "4/4 …"); it advances
   by itself, the round button pauses and plays, a dash jumps to its step; frames 2–4 match
   `start-demo-step2…4-light-*.png` except for the two added lines above; with Reduce
   Motion it does not start by itself. — test, browser.
7. **Owner's design choices are kept:** cards around the two choices and the results; the
   pill badge "Before registration. Before you pay."; the upload icon in a circle; the small
   uppercase section labels. — test, browser.
8. **Page 1 wording** as published, including "~30 seconds · No transcript needed" and "Your
   transcript PDF is read and discarded; nothing is stored." — test.
9. **Page 2 content.** "Spring 2027", "No transcript added"; 18 / 12 / 6; the six courses in
   order with code, title when known, credits and exactly one answer, worded as in the
   brief; the "Remember: …" line; "Add your transcript for an exact answer". The typing
   state shows "mgt 3" suggesting "MGT 321 Principles of Management" with 15 / 9 / 6.
   — test, browser.
10. **Truthfulness: Counts.** Every "Counts toward CIS" answer on every page, including the
    Start example and the demo, is followed by "unless you've already passed it or filled
    this slot". No "You already passed this", "This slot is already filled" or pace line on
    page 2. (Conflict b, the summary number, is the one listed exception.) — test, browser.
11. **Truthfulness: pace and plan.** "On pace for Spring 2029" is always followed by
    "Credits only — course order may add semesters."; the plan carries "Draft — Order not
    checked — your portal checks prerequisites"; nothing in progress or still needed reads
    Done; ECO 251 is In progress with "Repeat; the earlier D doesn't count." — test, browser.
12. **Page 3 content.** 81 credits left; about 16 credits a term; Counted 27 · In progress ·
    Fall 2026 13 · Left 81 · Total 121; the three pace controls; nine requirements with
    credits and status in words; each opened requirement lists its courses as in the M0
    brief; the sequence line; five semesters (18, 18, 15, 15, 15 = 81) with 27 entries, each
    with "Move later"; six open choices; MGT 462 last with "Always the final semester";
    "Print / save PDF for your advisor" and "Clear my data". — test, browser.
13. **Arithmetic.** 27 + 13 + 81 = 121; requirement credits match the curriculum file; the
    plan holds exactly the still-needed courses and picks; course titles match the
    curriculum file. — test.
14. **On every page:** "Curriculum last reviewed: Nov 2026", "Not an official degree audit.",
    "Report a mistake", "Not affiliated with Alabama State University", the name PathCheck,
    no university logo or school colors, no "ask your advisor". — test, browser.
15. **Colors.** Every color is a token with light, dark and print values; none is set
    anywhere else; the light and dark values equal the published design's. — test.
16. **Contrast.** WCAG AA in light and dark: text ≥ 4.5:1 on every background it sits on;
    control borders, marks and the focus ring ≥ 3:1. — test, browser spot-check.
17. **375px.** Nothing is clipped or overlapping and there is no sideways scroll, on every
    page, light and dark, including with every requirement opened. (The nav links are not
    shown on a phone: conflict h.) — browser.
18. **Keyboard.** Every link and control is reached with Tab in a sensible order and shows a
    visible focus ring; a requirement opens with Enter or Space. — browser, test (CSS).
19. **Controls.** Every control is a real `<a>`, `<button>`, `<input>`, `<select>` or
    `<summary>` with a visible or accessible label, at least 44px tall. — browser (measured),
    test (labels).
20. **Course codes** never break across lines, and are in JetBrains Mono with a slashed
    zero, including codes inside sentences. — test, browser.
21. **Plain project code.** No Figma Make component library, toast or icon pack; no new
    package dependency; no request to another site; fonts are the two open-licence files in
    `mockups/fonts/` with their licences. — test.
22. **Print.** Printing page 3 gives a white page without the top bar, the toggle, "Move
    later" or the action buttons, with every requirement opened. — test, browser (print
    emulation).
23. **No console errors** and no failed requests on any page. — browser.
24. **Conflicts are listed, not hidden.** Each item a–h above is visible in the mockups as
    described and appears in PR #4 under "Conflicts with the brief". Anything else the
    evaluator finds that conflicts with the brief and is not on that list is a defect.
    — browser, reviewer.

## Scores
The usual four (Functionality, Clarity, Visual design, Truthfulness) plus **Matches the
owner's design**, each 1–5. Below 4 on any is FAIL. The owner-approved design choices
(calibration, 2026-11-15) are not marked as generic or as banned elements. A conflict
listed in a–h and kept as designed is reported but is not, on its own, a FAIL.
