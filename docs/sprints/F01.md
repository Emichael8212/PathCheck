behavior: the major dropdown (one option), the add-course field, Add, the two filters,

## Where the mockups differ from the published design, and why
**Kept from the brief** (the owner's decision of 2026-11-15; unchanged since round 3):

1. Demo steps 2 and 3: "Counts toward CIS — Business core" is followed by "unless you've
   already passed it or filled this slot".
2. Demo step 4: "On pace for Spring 2029" is followed by "Credits only — course order may
   add semesters."
3. "Report a mistake" is a `mailto:` link to a placeholder address.
4. Dark mode follows the device setting.
5. The nav items and the two entry buttons are links.
6. On a phone the two nav links are shown, on a second row under the wordmark.

**The owner's answers** (2026-11-15):

- a. The "Your major" dropdown is kept: one option, "B.S. Computer Information Systems",
  pre-selected; no other or disabled majors.
- b. Page 2: under "12 Count toward CIS" the cell says "unless you've already passed them or
  filled their slots".
- c. The filter says "Doesn't count". No "unconfirmed", no "Can't confirm" answer.
- d. In progress and Still needed requirements show their courses without a tap. Done
  requirements are collapsed and open with "Show courses". Later the same day: In progress
  and Still needed requirements have the same "Show courses" / "Hide courses" toggle (they
  start open).
- Footer (same day): "Not affiliated with Alabama State University" is at the bottom right,
  level with the other three footer items.
- `features.json` F02 and F06 are reworded with the owner's approved wording (decision log).
- e. Two controls, "Fewest credits per term" (12) and "Most credits per term" (18), both
  editable, no hard limit; neutral notes under 12 and over 18.
- f. f1: the page-2 table header is normal-case. f2: no legend; the credit bar's parts are
  labelled directly. f3–f5: pill-shaped tabs, the indigo main button and the numbered chips
  stay.
- g. "Counts" is an outlined tick; "Done" is a filled tick. Five statuses, five shapes.
- h. 12 everywhere.
- i. The privacy sentence is "Your PDF is read, then deleted. Only your result stays in this
  browser.", next to each upload control.

**Small things the agent decided while applying the answers** (listed in the PR for the owner):

- The major reads "B.S. Computer Information Systems", as the owner wrote it; the published
  design had "B.S. - Computer Information Systems".
- The brief names no courses for the three Done requirements. So that "Show courses" shows
  something, the synthetic student has ORI 100; ENG 131 and ENG 132; BIO 127, BIOL 127,
  BIO 128, BIOL 128 and MAT 137 (titles and credits from the curriculum file).
- The line under "Requirements" is now "Credits counted / needed." (it used to end "Open a
  requirement for courses.", which is no longer true).
- Direct bar labels: "13 in progress · Fall 2026" above the hatched part; "27 counted", "81
  left" and "121 total" under the bar.
- The note wording under the steppers is the brief's: "Below 12 credits: below full-time;
  may affect scholarships." and "Over 18 credits: may cost extra."
- The two credit controls never cross: pushing one past the other takes it along. Neither
  goes below 1 credit, where its "−" is switched off. There is no upper stop.
- The page-2 table header keeps its quiet grey band; only its text changed to normal case.
- In progress and Still needed requirements start open; their toggle reads "Hide courses".
- Footer on narrow screens: the three left items wrap first and the affiliation line stays
  at the right, level with the first of them. Under about 630px wide (a phone) it drops
  below the other three, at the left.

19. **Controls.** Every control is a real `<a>`, `<button>`, `<input>`, `<select>` or

22. **Print.** Printing page 3 gives a white page without the top bar, the toggle, "Show

24. **Nothing open is hidden.** PR #4 has a descriptive title, and its body, in plain
