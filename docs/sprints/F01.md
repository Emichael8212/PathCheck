## What the student gets
Nothing yet. The owner gets three design directions, each showing the same three pages
with the same content: Start, the "Check my courses" result, and the "See what I have
left" result. Each direction has its own written rationale.

## User-visible change
Yes — static mockups under `mockups/`, opened from
http://127.0.0.1:5173/mockups/index.html. They are not the app. Only these things work:

## Content (fixed by the design brief; same in all three directions)
Every string, course, number and status on pages 1–3 comes from `docs/M0-DESIGN-BRIEF.md`
"The 3 pages". Course titles not listed there come from
`docs/curriculum/provisional-current.json`. Nothing else is added except:

  "Target semester", "Credits per term", "Summers", "Move later", "Pause the demo").
- Page 2 count line: the brief says "15 count toward CIS", but the four courses that count
  are 3 credits each, which is **12**; 15 would need BIO 230 or ENG 140 to count. The
  mockups show "12 count toward CIS" with the condition right beside it ("unless you've

- Page 2 credit notes: at 18 credits neither note applies. Both are shown, worded exactly

  question for the owner.
- Page 3 Done blocks (Orientation, Composition, Science & math): the brief names no
  courses for them, so the mockups show only the block name, credits and "Done". No
  disclosure, no invented course list.
- Pages 2 and 3 carry the "on every result" lines from `docs/BRIEF.md` §4: "Not an official
  degree audit", "Curriculum last reviewed: Nov 2026", "Report a mistake".
- Page 1 "how it works": three captioned frames (type your courses → see which count →
  see what's left) that fade in turn. No claims, numbers or testimonials.

## Design rules for this round
- **Apple's guidance, as read from the Human Interface Guidelines** (Materials, Layout,
  Toolbars, Buttons, Accessibility, Color; "Adopting Liquid Glass"):
  - Liquid Glass is a separate layer for controls and navigation that floats above the
    content. It is never used for content, never stacked glass on glass, and used sparingly.
  - Content scrolls under the bars; bars have no solid backing.
  - Buttons are capsules or concentric rounded shapes, at least 44×44; at most two
    prominent (tinted) actions per view; color on glass only to mark a primary action. On
    Start the two ways in carry equal weight: neither path is favoured.
  - Contrast meets WCAG AA; status uses a word plus a glyph shape, never color alone.
  - Reduce Transparency / Increase Contrast make the bars opaque; Reduce Motion stops the demo.
  - Apple's fonts and symbols are not embedded. Each direction picks its own type from
    open-licence fonts, shipped in `mockups/fonts/` with their licences, so the pages look
    the same on every device: A — IBM Plex Sans + IBM Plex Mono; B — Atkinson
    Hyperlegible; C — Barlow Condensed + Barlow, with JetBrains Mono for course codes.
- This lifts one ban from brief §8 (frosted glass), by the owner's instruction. The other
  bans stay: pill tab steppers, a card around everything, icon-in-circle feature lists,
  uppercase grey table headers, a default blue primary button, toasts, legends, multiple
  side panels.

## Acceptance checks
Automated checks run with `npm test` (`tests/mockups.test.js`). "Browser" is the evaluator
with Playwright at 375×667 and 1440×900, light and dark (`prefers-color-scheme`).

1. **Pages.** `mockups/index.html` links to three directions; each has `start.html`,
   `check.html`, `left.html`; Start links to the other two; every page links back to Start
   and to the index. Every page of every direction shows the name "PathCheck" and "Not
   affiliated with Alabama State University". Student data is synthetic only: no person's
   name, student ID or transcript text anywhere. — test, browser.
2. **Page 1 content.** The promise; the two ways in with their descriptions ("Check my
   courses" … "~30 seconds, no transcript needed"; "See what I have left" … "unofficial
   transcript PDF from the portal"); nothing to fill in; "Not an official degree audit.",
   "Curriculum last reviewed: Nov 2026", "Report a mistake" (a `mailto:` to a placeholder
   address), "Not affiliated with Alabama State University"; "The transcript PDF is read
   and discarded; nothing is stored." — test, browser.
3. **Page 1 demo.** A "how it works" sequence of three captioned frames with a labelled
   pause control; it does not move under Reduce Motion; it makes no claim beyond the
   brief; a frame that shows a "Counts" answer shows its condition, and no frame shows
   Done for something in progress. — test (markup, `prefers-reduced-motion` rule), browser.
4. **Page 2 content.** "Spring 2027"; the six courses in the brief's order, each with code,
   title when known (none for MKT 410 and BIO 230), "3 cr" and exactly one answer, worded
   exactly as in the brief; each of the four "Counts" answers is followed by "unless
   you've already passed it or filled this slot"; "18 credits"; "12 count toward CIS"
   with the condition beside it; the "Remember: …" line; both credit notes, attached to the
   18-credit total and not styled as warnings; "Add your transcript for an exact answer";
   "Not an official degree audit", "Curriculum last reviewed: Nov 2026" and a "Report a
   mistake" `mailto:` whose subject names the result. — test, browser.
5. **Page 2 controls.** A labelled text field showing "mgt 3" with an open suggestion
   offering "MGT 321 Principles of Management"; a Remove control per course that names its
   course. — test, browser.
6. **Page 2 truthfulness.** No "Counts" answer appears without the condition, in the
   per-course answers or in the "12 count toward CIS" summary line. The mark for "Counts" is not the mark for "Done" on page 3. No "You
   already passed this", "This slot is already filled" or pace line. — test, browser.
7. **"Doesn't count" is unmistakable, not alarming.** At 375px, a person glancing at page 2
   for two seconds can say which two courses don't count. The cue is a word plus a shape
   or structure, not color alone, and reads as information, not as an error or a warning
   siren. — browser (evaluator judgment, per direction).
8. **Page 3 content.** "81 credits left"; "On pace for Spring 2029"; "about 16 credits a
   term"; 27 counted, 13 in progress (Fall 2026), 121 total; "Credits only — course order
   may add semesters."; controls for target semester (Spring 2029), credits per term
   (12–18) and summers (off); the same three "on every result" lines as page 2. — test,
   browser.
9. **Page 3 blocks.** The nine blocks with the brief's counted/needed credits and status
   in words; the Done / In progress / Still needed courses listed exactly as in the brief,
   including "(repeat; the earlier D doesn't count)" on ECO 251 and the "1 of …" picks;
   remaining courses of In progress and Still needed blocks are visible without opening
   anything; Done blocks show name, credits and "Done" only; if a direction hides any
   course list, it is in a native disclosure with a visible cue; the
   line "Still needed: HIS 132 or ENG 210 to complete a history or literature sequence."
   — test, browser.
10. **Page 3 truthfulness.** No block or course that is in progress or still needed reads
    Done; ECO 251 is In progress, never counted. Counted credits total 27, needed credits
    121, in-progress credits 13 (ECO 251, HUM 103, ACT 214, MGT 202 at 3, PED 114 at 1).
    The plan's 27 entries are exactly the still-needed courses and picks (81 credits); no
    Done or in-progress course is in the plan. — test.
11. **Page 3 plan.** Labelled "Draft — Order not checked — your portal checks
    prerequisites"; the five semesters with the brief's entries and credits (18, 18, 15,
    15, 15 = 81); MGT 462 Business Policies in Spring 2029; a "Move later" control on every
    entry that names its entry; "pick" and "elective" entries look like open choices, not
    courses; "Print / save PDF for your advisor" and "Clear my data". — test, browser.
12. **"What's left" at a glance on a phone.** At 375×667 the first screen of page 3 shows
    "81 credits left" and the pace answer without scrolling. — test (strings), browser.
13. **Status cues.** Done, In progress, Still needed, Counts and Doesn't count each have a
    word and their own non-color cue (five different shapes or patterns). — test (five
    distinct marks), browser.
14. **Course codes.** No code breaks across lines at 375px; codes are set in a face where
    0/O and 1/l differ (slashed or dotted zero): IBM Plex Mono, Atkinson Hyperlegible,
    JetBrains Mono, loaded from `mockups/fonts/`. Every course code uses the code face,
    including codes inside sentences, the "1 of …" lists and the plan entries. — test (`white-space: nowrap`, the font
    files exist and are referenced), browser (a screenshot of "CIS 410" and "MGT 201" in
    each direction).
15. **Controls.** Every interactive element is a real `<a>`, `<button>`, `<input>`,
    `<select>` or `<summary>` with a visible or accessible label, reachable with Tab, with
    a visible focus ring, and at least 44×44 CSS px. — browser (measured), test (labels).
16. **Colors.** Every color is a named variable with a light and a dark value; no color
    value outside the variable blocks; no black-and-gold scheme. — test.
17. **Contrast.** WCAG AA: text ≥ 4.5:1, large text and non-text marks ≥ 3:1, in light and
    dark, including text on glass measured against the darkest and lightest thing that can
    scroll under it. — test (variables, with glass composited), browser spot-check.
18. **Liquid Glass used as Apple describes.** Glass appears only on floating controls and
    navigation (bars, the suggestion menu); never on content; never glass on glass; content
    scrolls under the bars; with Reduce Transparency or Increase Contrast, or without
    `backdrop-filter` support, the bars are opaque and everything stays readable. — test
    (which selectors use `backdrop-filter`; the fallbacks exist), browser.
19. **Distinct, and not a template.** The three directions differ in layout idea, type and
    palette (not recolors of one layout), each is tied to this problem and these students,
    and none looks like a generic SaaS/AI template. — browser (calibration rule 1), test
    (palette and typeface differ).
20. **Still banned.** No pill tab stepper, card around everything, icon-in-circle feature
    list, uppercase grey table header, default blue primary button, toast, legend or
    multiple side panels; nothing from brief §6; no "ask your advisor"; no university logo.
    — test (strings, CSS), browser.
21. **Layout.** At 375px and 1440px nothing is hidden, clipped or overlapping and there is
    no horizontal scroll, light and dark; the desktop layout uses the width (it is not the
    phone layout stretched or stranded in a narrow strip). — browser.
22. **Rationale and questions.** Each direction has a rationale of about half a page
    covering the three points the brief asks for; the open content questions are listed in
    one place. — test (present), reviewer (substance).
23. **No console errors** on any page, and no failed font or file requests. — browser.
24. **Print.** Printing page 3 gives an opaque, readable page on white: no glass bars, no
    controls that only make sense on screen, every block and the whole plan present.
    — test (`@media print` rules), browser (print emulation).
