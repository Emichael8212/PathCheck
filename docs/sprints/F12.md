# F12 — Flow A with a transcript: exact answers

Milestone: M1 · Branch: `feat/F12-exact-answers` · Brief sections: §4 Flow A steps 2–3, §5

## What the student gets
On "Check my courses" a student can add their transcript. The answers then stop being
conditional: a course they already passed says so, a course whose place is already taken
says so, and a course that fills an open requirement counts, with no "unless".

## User-visible change
Yes.

## What is built
- **On "Check my courses"** (`#/check`), as in the approved design: "No transcript added"
  at the right of the heading, and under the list the card "Add your transcript for an
  exact answer" with the privacy sentence and the button "Choose transcript PDF". The
  button opens the same file picker as "Browse files".
- **The same upload and review step as F11.** A PDF that is taken opens "Check what was
  read" (`#/left`). "Continue" there brings the student back to "Check my courses" when
  the PDF was chosen on that page, and the review says so under the button ("You’ll go
  back to “Check my courses”."); chosen on Start or on "See what I have left", it stays
  there as today. A file that is refused when chosen on "Check my courses" stays there:
  its sentence is shown on the card, the keyboard on the card's button. There is one
  transcript for the visit, used by both flows.
- **With a transcript** (after "Continue", with at least one row taken on and no row
  still needing fixing):
  - the heading note says "Transcript added";
  - the card says that the answers use the transcript, names the file, repeats the line
    of counts from the review ("14 rows taken on. 1 left out because it still needs
    fixing."), and offers "Use another transcript" with "Choosing another transcript
    forgets your corrections." That button forgets the transcript and the page is back
    to the conditional answers and the "Add your transcript" card;
  - each answer is exact (table below) and no "Counts" carries the condition; the
    condition under "Count toward CIS" in the summary is dropped.
- **With a transcript that still has rows needing fixing** (the student continued
  anyway; a row left out as "Not on my transcript" is not one of them): the transcript is
  used, so "You already passed this", "This slot is already filled" and the in-progress
  sentence are shown, but nothing is called exact. Every "Counts" keeps its condition,
  the summary keeps its condition, the note says "Transcript added", and the card says
  why: "<m> rows of your transcript still need fixing, so these answers are not exact
  yet." with a link "Fix them" that opens "Check what was read" again, corrections and
  left-out rows kept; its "Continue" comes back to "Check my courses". A row that could
  not be checked may be the very course that was typed.
- **While the transcript is still being checked** (uploaded, "Continue" not yet pressed):
  the answers stay conditional, the note says "No transcript added", and the card says
  "Your transcript is waiting to be checked." with a link "Check what was read".
- **Placement** (`src/placement.ts`): typed courses become a third, weaker state,
  *planned*. A planned course takes only a place that no passed and no in-progress course
  holds. Among planned courses the rules of F08 stay: the sequence course wins its pick,
  the first typed electives take the open elective places. Without a transcript all typed
  courses are planned, and every answer of F08 stays word for word.

## The answer for a typed course, with a transcript
"On the transcript" means: among the rows taken on by the review (F11), after repeats (the
highest grade counts, F03).

| The course… | Answer | Mark |
|---|---|---|
| is in no curriculum list and no elective by the rule (whatever the transcript says) | "Doesn't count toward CIS — not in the CIS curriculum" | Doesn't count |
| is CIS 304 or CIS 415 (whatever the transcript says) | "Doesn't count toward CIS — <code> isn't in the current CIS curriculum." | Doesn't count |
| is passed on the transcript (a grade of C or better, or transfer / AP / CLEP credit) | "You already passed this" | Doesn't count |
| is in progress on the transcript (no grade yet) | "In progress on your transcript — it doesn't count again." (agent's words) | Doesn't count |
| is on the transcript only below C, or with W or I; or is not on it | goes on as below | |
| fills a required place or a pick that no course holds | "Counts toward CIS — <block>" (no condition) | Counts |
| is an elective and a place is open | the elective answers of F08 (no condition) | Counts |
| belongs to a pick held by a course of the transcript | "This slot is already filled" | Doesn't count |
| is an elective and all places are held by courses of the transcript | "This slot is already filled" | Doesn't count |
| loses its place to another course of the student's own list (alone or together with courses of the transcript) | the sentences of F08, naming the courses that hold it | Doesn't count |
| is FLS 161, MGT 220 or MGT 203 and the student is not a legacy student | "Doesn't count toward CIS. Legacy students only." (F08) | Doesn't count |
| is PSY 251, its pick is held by a course of the transcript, and the student is not a legacy student | "This slot is already filled" (the real reason; F08's "Legacy students only" stays for a pick held by the student's own list) | Doesn't count |
| is PSY 251, its pick is held, and the student is a legacy student | an elective place if one is open: "Counts toward CIS: elective (legacy)"; otherwise the full-electives answer | |
| is HIS 132 or ENG 210, its pick is held by another course of the transcript, and neither sequence is otherwise on its way | **waits for the owner** (below). Until then: "Needed for the <name of the sequence>. Its credits don’t add to your degree total." | no mark; in neither number |

**The last row is an open question to the owner** (recorded in `docs/decision-log.md`,
asked on 2026-11-16). That the course is still needed follows from the sequence rule
(confirmed 2026-11-15): the degree needs one of the two sequences whatever fills the
picks, so "This slot is already filled" would send the student away from a course they
must take. But the owner also said the sequence adds no credits, and with the pick
already held the course's credits fill no place: how to word that, and in which number
its credits go, is the owner's to say. Until the answer, the course is never shown as
"Counts" and never as "This slot is already filled": it shows the cautious sentence
above, drawn without a mark, and its credits are in the total only. A sequence is "on
its way" when its course that sits in a pick (HIS 132; ENG 210) is passed, in progress,
typed and holding its pick, or typed earlier in the list. (The other course of each sequence is required anyway.)

## Decided here (recorded in `docs/decision-log.md`)
- **"Not in the curriculum" comes before "You already passed this".** It is true with or
  without a transcript, and it is what the student most needs to know.
- **A course in progress on the transcript** gets a sentence of the agent's: "In progress
  on your transcript — it doesn't count again." It is in the "Don't count" number.
- **"You already passed this", "This slot is already filled" and the in-progress sentence
  are drawn as "Doesn't count"** (the square mark, the tinted row, the "Doesn't count"
  filter, the "Don't count" number): none of them adds anything toward the degree.
- **"This slot is already filled" only when the transcript alone fills the place.** When a
  course of the student's own list holds it, F08's sentences say which.
- **A sequence course that is still needed although its pick is held** is an open
  question to the owner; the cautious sentence stands in until the answer (the table's
  last row).
- **Rows that still need fixing keep every answer conditional** (contract review, round
  1): an unchecked row may be the course that was typed.
- **PSY 251 against a pick held by the transcript**, for a student who is not a legacy
  student: "This slot is already filled".
- **The legacy question with a transcript.** The answer can now change a course that is
  not a legacy course (legacy courses on the transcript take elective places only for a
  legacy student). When no typed legacy course shows the question, the first row whose
  answer depends on it shows it, with a line that says why: "Your transcript has FLS 161,
  which counts only for legacy students." (the legacy courses passed or in progress on
  the transcript, by name). The others wait, as in F08. A typed legacy course that is
  already passed says "You already passed this" whatever the answer.
- **The reminder about HIS 132 / ENG 210** is not shown when the transcript has one of
  them passed or in progress.
- **A transcript with no row taken on** changes nothing: the answers stay conditional.
- **The review happens on `#/left`**, under "See what I have left" in the navigation,
  also when the PDF was chosen on "Check my courses". One review step, not two.
- **Wording by the agent**, listed in the pull request: "Transcript added"; "Your answers
  use your transcript (<file>)."; "Your transcript is waiting to be checked."; "Check what
  was read" (the link); "Fix them" / "Fix it"; "No row of your transcript (<file>) was
  taken on, so these answers don’t use it."; "You’ll go back to “Check my courses”."; the
  in-progress sentence; the not-exact-yet sentence; the line under the legacy question; the stand-in
  sentence for the sequence.

## Acceptance checks
Tests are in `tests/placement.test.ts` (`placement`), `tests/answers.test.ts`
(`answers`), `tests/check.test.ts` (`check`, the page as text), `tests/check-page.test.ts`
(`check-page`, the simulated browser) and `tests/left-page.test.ts` (`left-page`).
"Evaluator" means checked in a real browser on the development server with the two
made-up transcripts.

1. **Optional.** Without a transcript the page works exactly as before: every answer of
   F08 and every number of F09, word for word, with the condition. The heading note says
   "No transcript added"; the card shows its title, the privacy sentence and "Choose
   transcript PDF". — every earlier test of `answers`, `check` and `check-page` unchanged
   in what it expects; `check` "offers the transcript as an option".
2. **The same upload and review.** "Choose transcript PDF" opens the file picker; the
   chosen PDF opens "Check what was read" with every behaviour of F11, and under
   "Continue" the line that says it goes back to "Check my courses". "Continue" returns
   there, the keyboard on its heading, the typed courses still there. From Start or
   `#/left` the line is not shown and "Continue" stays on `#/left`. A file that is
   refused (not a PDF, too large, not one of the made-up transcripts, cannot be opened)
   leaves the student on "Check my courses": its sentence is on the card and is read
   out, the keyboard is on "Choose transcript PDF", and a good file afterwards is taken.
   — `check-page` "adds a transcript and comes back", "says on the card why a file was
   not taken"; `left-page` unchanged; evaluator.
3. **Already passed.** A typed course passed on the transcript shows "You already passed
   this": by grade, as transfer / AP credit, and after a repeat (F then B). — `answers`
   "says a passed course is already passed".
4. **Slot filled.** A typed course of a pick that a passed or in-progress course of the
   transcript holds shows "This slot is already filled"; so does an elective when three
   electives of the transcript hold the places. — `answers` "says a slot is filled by the
   transcript".
5. **Counts, exactly.** A typed course that fills an open required place, an open pick or
   an open elective place shows its "Counts" answer with no condition line, and the
   summary's "Count toward CIS" has no condition. — `answers` "counts a course that fills
   an open place"; `check` "drops the condition with a transcript".
6. **In progress.** A typed course that is in progress on the transcript never shows
   "Counts": it shows the in-progress sentence, and its credits are in "Don't count". —
   `answers` "does not count a course in progress again".
7. **Below C, W, I.** A typed course that the transcript has only below C, or with W or
   I, is answered as if it were not on the transcript: it counts when its place is open.
   A course with an F and a later in-progress row is in progress. — `answers` "counts a
   course that must be repeated".
8. **A planned course never takes a place from the transcript.** A typed sequence course
   does not push a passed or in-progress course out of its pick; typed electives take
   only the places the transcript leaves, in typed order; a place held by the student's
   own list is answered with F08's sentences naming the holders (transcript and typed
   together for electives). — `placement` "places planned courses after the transcript's";
   `answers` "names the courses of the list that hold a place".
9. **Never in the curriculum.** A course that is in no list, and CIS 304 / CIS 415, show
   their "Doesn't count" sentence whatever the transcript says, passed included. —
   `answers` "keeps the curriculum's no before the transcript".
10. **The sequence** (the stand-in, until the owner answers). With HIS 131 and GEO 206
    passed and no literature sequence on its way, typed HIS 132 shows "Needed for the
    history sequence. Its credits don’t add to your degree total.": never "Counts",
    never "This slot is already filled", no mark, its credits in the total only. With ENG
    210 passed or in progress, or typed before it, HIS 132 shows "This slot is already
    filled". With HIS 132 typed before ENG 210 (both picks held), HIS 132 is the needed
    one and ENG 210 shows "This slot is already filled". The same the other way round.
    When the pick is open, the course simply counts toward its block. The reminder line
    is shown only when neither course is on the transcript (passed or in progress) or in
    the list. — `answers` "does not send away a sequence course that is still needed",
    "reminds of the sequence only when it is open".
11. **The legacy question.** With a transcript that has a legacy course passed (FLS 161)
    and two elective places otherwise open, typing two electives shows the question on
    the first row whose answer depends on it, with the line naming FLS 161 under it, and
    "Answer “Are you a legacy CIS student?” to see this." on the others; Yes and No
    change the answers and the numbers; the answer is remembered, is not asked again, and
    can be changed with "Change". A typed legacy course that is already passed shows "You
    already passed this" and no question. Typed PSY 251 against a pick held by the
    transcript shows the question; on No "This slot is already filled"; on Yes "Counts
    toward CIS: elective (legacy)" while a place is open. No verdict that the answer
    could change is shown before it is given. For F05, on screen: the question, Yes and
    No are `LEGACY_QUESTION`, `LEGACY_YES` and `LEGACY_NO` exactly; the answer survives a
    reload (shown with a typed legacy course, since a transcript does not survive one
    before F19); keyboard only; 375px. — `answers` "asks the legacy question when the
    transcript makes it matter", "answers PSY 251 against a pick the transcript holds";
    `check-page` "asks the legacy question with a transcript"; evaluator.
12. **The numbers.** Total, "Count toward CIS" and "Don't count" follow the exact
    answers; the under-12 and over-18 notes are unchanged. The credits of a course that
    is "needed for a sequence" (the stand-in of check 10) are in the total and in neither
    count, so for any list: total = count + don't count + waiting + needed for a
    sequence. — `check` "drops the condition with a transcript"; `check-page` "adds a
    transcript and comes back"; `credits` "adds up for any list" (F09's test, extended).
    `answers` "does not send away a sequence course that is still needed" checks the

13. **Still being checked; no rows; another transcript.** Before "Continue" the answers

14. **Both flows agree.** The exact answers are worked out by `placeCourses`, the same

15. **Nothing is said to count that may not.** For random lists of 1 to 8 typed courses
