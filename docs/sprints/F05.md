## What is built
- `src/legacy.ts`:
  - `LEGACY_QUESTION`, the question's exact words, and the two answers "Yes" and "No", so
    every screen shows the same text;
  - `legacyQuestionNeeded(codes, curriculum)`: whether any of the given course codes is one
    of the curriculum's legacy-only courses;
  - `readLegacyAnswer(storage)`, `writeLegacyAnswer(storage, answer)` and
    `clearLegacyAnswer(storage)`: the answer kept in the browser's `localStorage`, under one
    key, `pathcheck.legacy`. The answer is Yes, No, or not given yet.
- `placeCourses(standings, curriculum, { legacy })` (`src/placement.ts`, from F04) takes the
  answer:
  - **Yes:** a legacy-only course that was passed or is in progress can fill an elective
    place, like any other elective (three places, 3 credits each; passed before in progress,
    then transcript order). A grade below C, a W or an I still counts nothing.
  - **No, or not answered yet:** it fills no elective place, exactly as before this feature.
- **PSY 251 is listed twice**: as a Social sciences pick, and as a legacy-only elective.
  For a legacy student it is placed where it helps most (see "Decided here").
- The four courses, their titles and credits come from the curriculum data
  (`electives.legacyOnly`); none is written in `src/`.

## Decided here (recorded in `docs/decision-log.md`)
- **Not answered yet is treated as No** when counting: a legacy-only course never fills an
  elective place until the student has said Yes. A screen, though, must tell the two apart:
  while the answer is missing it asks the question and shows no verdict for those courses
  (carried forward to the screens).
- **"Where it helps most" for PSY 251**, an extension of the F04 reading. With legacy Yes,
  the placement is the one where PSY 251 is only a pick course (the other three legacy
  courses being electives). It changes only if treating PSY 251 as an elective instead gives
  strictly more credits done, or the same credits done and strictly more in progress. This
  only chooses between two places the curriculum already gives that course; it never
  counts a course the rules would not count.
- **If the browser's storage cannot be used** (private browsing, storage switched off), the
  answer is not remembered: reading gives "not given yet", writing does nothing, and nothing
  fails. Screens therefore keep the answer in memory for the visit and never rely on
  reading it back (carried forward).
- **F05 stays open until a screen shows the question** (see "How F05 is finished").

## Acceptance checks
Tests are in `tests/legacy.test.ts`; made-up students only.

1. **When the question is needed.** `legacyQuestionNeeded` is true when the codes include
   PSY 251, FLS 161, MGT 220 or MGT 203, however typed ("psy251", "FLS-161"), and false for
   any other list: an empty one; look-alikes (PSY 250, MGT 204, FLS 162); and junk, which
   never throws (`""`, `"hello"`, `"PSY"`, `"PSY 2511"`, junk mixed with GEO 206). It follows
   the curriculum it is given: with a test curriculum whose legacy-only list is different,
   it answers for that list. — "asks only when one of the four courses appears", "reads the
   legacy courses from the curriculum".
2. **The exact words.** The question is "Are you a legacy CIS student?" and the answers are
   "Yes" and "No". — "uses the brief's wording".
3. **Yes: the three that are only electives.** FLS 161, MGT 220 and MGT 203 passed, legacy
   Yes: each fills an elective place as done (9 credits), marked as an elective by the
   legacy rule. One in progress fills its place as in progress. — "counts legacy-only
   courses as electives for a legacy student".
4. **No, and not answered.** The same courses with legacy No, and with no answer: none
   fills a place, each is left over with the reason "legacy-only elective", 0 credits. For
   any transcript, the placement with No, with no answer, and with no option at all are
   identical, and every existing test in `tests/placement.test.ts` passes unchanged.
   — "does not count them for a student who is not legacy", "treats no answer as No".
5. **Grades still apply.** Legacy Yes with FLS 161 graded D, MGT 220 with W, MGT 203 with I
   and PSY 251 with D: none fills any place and no credits are counted. — "does not count a
   legacy course that was not passed".
6. **Transfer credit.** FLS 161 as a transfer row whose grade column shows "TR": with
   legacy Yes it fills an elective place as done; with No or no answer it is left over with
   "legacy-only elective" and 0 credits. — "asks the legacy question of transfer credit too".
7. **Three electives, no more.** Legacy Yes with FLS 161, MGT 220, MGT 203, FIN 201 and
   MKT 410 all passed: three fill the places in transcript order, two are left over with
   "electives full"; elective credits are 9. — "still stops at three electives".
8. **PSY 251 alone.** Passed, with nothing else in the Social sciences pick: it fills the
   pick, and the placements with Yes, No and no answer are identical. — "keeps PSY 251 in
   its pick when the pick is free".
9. **PSY 251 where it helps most.** Legacy Yes:
   - PSY 251 and GEO 206 both passed, in either order: GEO 206 fills the pick, PSY 251 an
     elective place; 6 credits done. With No the same transcript counts 3.
   - PSY 251, HIS 131 and HIS 132 all passed: HIS 132 fills the pick, PSY 251 an elective
     place; 9 credits done; the history sequence is done.
   - PSY 251 in progress and GEO 206 passed: GEO 206 fills the pick as done, PSY 251 an
     elective place as in progress.
   - PSY 251 passed, HIS 131 passed, HIS 132 in progress. With Yes: HIS 132 fills the pick
     as in progress, PSY 251 an elective place as done; 6 done, 3 in progress. With No:
     PSY 251 fills the pick, HIS 132 is left over, "pick already filled" by PSY 251; 6 done,
     0 in progress. The sequence is in progress either way.
   — "moves PSY 251 to an elective place when that counts more".
10. **PSY 251 when moving it gains nothing.** Legacy Yes, three other electives passed
    (FIN 201, MKT 410, CIS 499), so the elective places are full:
    - PSY 251 then GEO 206, both passed: PSY 251 fills the pick; GEO 206 is left over, "pick
      already filled" by PSY 251; the three electives are FIN 201, MKT 410, CIS 499; 12
      credits done. The same as the placement with PSY 251 only a pick.
    - GEO 206 then PSY 251, both passed: GEO 206 fills the pick; PSY 251 is left over, "pick
      already filled" by GEO 206; the same three electives; 12 credits done.
    - PSY 251 passed, GEO 206 in progress: PSY 251 stays in the pick as done; GEO 206 is left
      over, "pick already filled" by PSY 251; the three electives are FIN 201, MKT 410,
      CIS 499; 12 credits done, 0 in progress. A done course is never traded for one in
      progress.
    - PSY 251 in progress, GEO 206 passed: GEO 206 fills the pick; PSY 251 is left over,
      "pick already filled" by GEO 206; 12 credits done, 0 in progress.
    — "leaves PSY 251 in its pick when moving it gains nothing".
11. **Never worse, never double.** Over a few hundred made-up transcripts drawn at random
    with a fixed seed (the seed is printed if a case fails), including the four legacy
    courses, HIS 132 and ENG 210, rows with D, W and I, and transfer rows:
    - no answer always gives the same placement as No;
    - with Yes, credits done are never lower than with No, and credits done plus in
      progress are never lower;
    - the Yes placement is the better of the two arrangements (PSY 251 only a pick, PSY 251
      only an elective) by the rule above;
    - no course fills two places, and elective credits never exceed 9;
    - a student with none of the four courses gets the same placement whatever the answer.
    — "never counts less for a legacy student", "changes nothing for a student without the
    four courses".
12. **Remembering the answer.** After `writeLegacyAnswer(storage, true)`, `readLegacyAnswer`
    gives Yes; after writing No it gives No; the student can change it any number of times;
    `clearLegacyAnswer` puts it back to "not given yet". An answer written and then read
    through a fresh call, as after a page reload, is the same. Before anything is written,
    and when the stored value is not one this code wrote, it is "not given yet". Only the
    key `pathcheck.legacy` is used, and nothing else in storage is touched. Writing
    anything that is not a real Yes or No keeps nothing and changes nothing (added in QA).
    — "remembers the answer and lets it be changed", "ignores a stored value it does not
    recognise", "keeps only a real Yes or No".
13. **Storage that cannot be used.** With a storage that throws on every call (as some
    browsers do in private mode), and with none at all, reading gives "not given yet" and
    writing and clearing do nothing; none of them throws. `browserStorage()` gives the
    browser's storage, or nothing when the browser refuses even the mention of it (added
    in review). — "works without storage", "works when the browser refuses any mention of
    its storage", "uses the browser's storage when there is one".
